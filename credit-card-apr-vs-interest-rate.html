Credit Card APR vs Interest Rate

APR expresses an annualized rate, while an actual interest charge depends on the balance, timing and terms for a particular account.

The short answer

Check the purchase APR, cash-advance APR, promotional terms, grace-period conditions and the statement calculation method.

What to check

Compare the current statement, agreement or offer before acting. Focus on the amount owed, APR, fees, required payment, due date and any promotional or service terms that apply to your specific accounts.
