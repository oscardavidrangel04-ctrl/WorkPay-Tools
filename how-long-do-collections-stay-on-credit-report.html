How Long Do Collections Stay on Your Credit Report?

Most collection accounts can appear for roughly seven years from the delinquency that led to collection. The reporting clock is not restarted simply because a collector buys the debt or you make a payment.

When do collections fall off?

For a collection or charge-off, federal reporting limits are tied to the original delinquency that preceded the collection. The statutory framework generally uses seven years plus a 180-day period from that delinquency. Bureau display and removal may occur earlier. Find the original creditor account and date of first delinquency, not just the date the collector opened its account.

Does paying restart the reporting clock?

Payment may update the balance and status, but it does not create a new first-delinquency date for the same collection. A paid collection can still appear until the applicable reporting period ends. See what a pay-for-delete offer does and does not promise.

How to check an old collection

Compare all three reports. Record the original creditor, account identifier, first delinquency, collection balance and current owner. If dates or ownership are wrong, dispute the exact error and provide supporting records.

Do not confuse reporting with collection rights

The credit-reporting period and the time limit for a collector to sue are different legal questions. State law and the facts of the account matter for the latter; do not assume a debt is uncollectible just because it no longer appears on a report.

Frequently asked questions

When do collections fall off a credit report?

Usually around seven years after the delinquency that led to the collection, subject to the federal reporting rules and the bureau record.

Does paying a collection remove it immediately?

No. Payment can update the balance or status, but does not automatically remove an accurate collection.

Does a new collector restart the seven-year period?

A transfer to a new collector does not change the original delinquency that controls the reporting period for the same debt.
