Three-Paycheck Months

Workers paid every two weeks usually receive 26 paychecks per year. Because 26 checks do not divide evenly across 12 months, a standard biweekly calendar generally produces two months with three paychecks.

Reviewed September 15, 2026

Why three paychecks occur in a month

A biweekly payday repeats every 14 days. Most months contain two paydays, but when the first payday falls early enough, two more 14-day intervals can fit in the same month. For example, paydays on the 1st, 15th and 29th create a three-paycheck month.

Which months have three paychecks?

There is no single answer for every worker or employer. The exact months depend on the first payday and the employer’s calendar. To identify yours, list every payday at 14-day intervals and count how many fall inside each calendar month.

This is why a generic list of “three-paycheck months in 2026” can be misleading without a starting payday. Two employees on different biweekly schedules can have different three-check months.

Three paychecks vs extra annual salary

A three-paycheck month does not create an extra annual salary for a salaried employee whose annual pay is divided into 26 checks. It changes the monthly cash-flow pattern. Two-check months contain two portions of the annual salary; three-check months contain three.

Biweekly vs semimonthly schedules

Biweekly pay normally means 26 checks and can create three-paycheck months. Semimonthly pay means two checks per month and 24 checks per year, so it normally does not. Compare the schedules in the biweekly vs semimonthly pay guide.

Planning for a three-paycheck month

If regular monthly expenses are built around two checks, the third check can provide additional cash flow in that month. It is still part of annual gross pay, and deductions may differ depending on employer payroll settings. Review the actual pay statement before assigning the entire check to another purpose.

Three-Paycheck Months FAQ

How many three-paycheck months are in a year?

A standard 26-paycheck biweekly year generally creates two months with three checks. Some payroll calendars can occasionally produce 27 paydays in a year.

Does everyone have the same three-paycheck months?

No. The months depend on the employer’s payday calendar and the first payday in the sequence.

Do semimonthly employees get three-paycheck months?

Normally no. Semimonthly means two checks per month.

Is the third paycheck a bonus?

No. It is a regular paycheck occurring in a month that contains three scheduled biweekly paydays.
