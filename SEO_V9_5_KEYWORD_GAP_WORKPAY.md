## Method

SEMrush Keyword Gap compared WorkPay with four verified SERP counterparts for the credit/debt topics being evaluated: `financecruncher.com`, `creditplainly.com`, `creditloancalculator.com`, and `payoffroute.com`. They were selected from live SERPs for credit-utilization and debt-snowball calculator queries; they are topic-level comparables, not asserted to be whole-site competitors.

The report date shown by SEMrush was September 16, 2026. WorkPay had 15 organic keywords in this data set. As a result, the `Missing` and `Weak` tabs both returned zero rows, while `Untapped` contained 380 rows. This is a coverage/indexing limitation of the young domain, not evidence that there are no content gaps.

## Changes implemented

1. Refined the calculator title, meta description, H1 and opening answer around the two exact calculator variants.
2. Kept one canonical calculator URL, avoiding a duplicate `credit-utilization-ratio-calculator` page.
3. Repositioned the existing guide for definition/formula intent, added the formula and a contextual HTML link to the calculator.
4. Expanded the guide FAQ/schema with the calculator question while preserving its separate educational intent.

## Deliberate exclusions

No credit-report, credit-score-estimator, Equifax/TransUnion, EMI, loan, or tax URL was created from this data. Those terms either have a distinct product/regulatory intent, fall outside the current cluster, or would require a separate evidence-backed brief.
