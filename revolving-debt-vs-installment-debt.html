Debt and credit planning

Revolving Debt vs Installment Debt

Revolving accounts generally have a credit limit and reusable capacity; installment accounts usually have a fixed principal and scheduled payments.

The short answer

Review the contract, APR, repayment term, fees and whether the balance can be borrowed again after a payment.

What to check

Compare the current statement, agreement or offer before acting. Focus on the amount owed, APR, fees, required payment, due date and any promotional or service terms that apply to your specific accounts.
