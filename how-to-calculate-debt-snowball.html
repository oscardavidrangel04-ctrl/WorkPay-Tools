How to Calculate a Debt Snowball Payment

Learn the payment-allocation idea behind a debt snowball plan.

The basic method

A debt snowball plan generally directs extra money to the smallest selected balance while required minimums continue on other debts. Actual interest, fees and payment timing affect payoff results.

What to verify

Use the written policy, statement, contract or plan document for the real terms. A simple formula cannot account for every timing rule, fee, benefit or eligibility condition.

Practical sequence

List each balance and its minimum payment, keep all minimums current, and apply the extra amount to the selected smallest balance. Recalculate after each payoff because balances and required payments change.

Question to check

Is snowball always the lowest-cost method?

Not necessarily. It prioritizes balance order; an avalanche method prioritizes APR.
