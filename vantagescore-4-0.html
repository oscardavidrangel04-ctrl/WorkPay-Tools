What VantageScore 4.0 means

The label identifies the model version used to calculate a score. It does not identify the credit bureau or calculation date, which should be checked separately.

VantageScore 4.0 vs 3.0

Both use a 300â€“850 range, but their model methods differ. Scores from the two versions should not be treated as identical even when based on the same report.

VantageScore 4.0 vs FICO

They are different model families. A lender selected score can differ from a consumer score because of model, version, bureau and timing.

How to check your score label

Look for the model name and version, bureau, score range and date. If a service omits those details, avoid assuming what the number represents.
