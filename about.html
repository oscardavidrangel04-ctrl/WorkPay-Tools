About WorkPay Tools

WorkPay Tools is a focused collection of browser-based pay and work-hour calculators.

Reviewed August 14, 2026

How calculations are built

Each calculator starts with a narrow question and a visible formula. Worked examples are included so a visitor can independently check the arithmetic. The calculators run in the browser and do not require an account.

How rule-sensitive pages are reviewed

When a page discusses federal overtime, compensable hours or the regular rate, WorkPay Tools links to primary U.S. Department of Labor material. We distinguish the calculator's simple mathematical model from a legal determination for a specific worker.

Editorial principles

We aim to

  • Show formulas and assumptions.
  • Use plain language.
  • Cite primary government sources where appropriate.
  • State important limitations.
  • Update pages when the underlying rules or methodology change.

We do not claim to

  • Replace payroll records.
  • Determine employee exemption status.
  • Provide legal, tax or financial advice.
  • Guarantee a specific paycheck.

Corrections

If you spot a calculation, wording or source issue, use the contact page and include the calculator URL and non-sensitive example inputs.
