# WorkPay Tools V9.0

## Semrush-led high-volume expansion

This release uses the supplied U.S. Semrush research to strengthen high-value salary, raise, overtime and income topics while preserving the validated V8.9 technical SEO baseline.

## New URLs

- `/how-to-ask-for-a-raise-at-work` — career-intent guide connected to the pay-raise calculator.
- `/what-is-gross-pay` — definition, formulas, examples and related calculators.
- `/what-is-net-pay` — take-home-pay definition and gross-vs-net distinction.

## Calculator improvements

- Salary Increase Calculator now includes an optional inflation comparison.
- Added weekly and approximate hourly increase outputs.
- Uses the compounded real-change formula: `(1 + raise rate) / (1 + inflation rate) - 1`.
- Clarifies that the inflation value is a user-entered comparison, not a forecast.

## Content and architecture

- Strengthened the `$30/hour` and `$40/hour` pages with gross-pay context.
- Connected annual-income, weekly-pay and biweekly-pay pages to gross/net definitions.
- Added contextual links from the calculator directory, homepage and raise/overtime hub.
- Preserved separate search intent to avoid duplicate pages for existing calculators and conversion examples.

## Technical consistency

- Corrected the site-wide calculator count to 19.
- Added Time and a Half as item 19 in the calculator-directory schema.
- Labeled the homepage 15-item schema list as featured rather than complete.
- Added all three new canonical URLs to `sitemap.xml` with `lastmod` 2026-09-13.

## Deliberately excluded

- Ambiguous phrases such as `day paid`, `prevailing` and `annual wage supplement` were not targeted because they do not cleanly match the site's U.S. pay-calculator intent.
