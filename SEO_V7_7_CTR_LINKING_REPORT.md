## 2. SERP patterns observed

Current competing results in this search space frequently use:

- Exact calculator type near the start of the title.
- Specific task language such as “with breaks”, “time-and-a-half”, “annual salary”, “hourly rate”, and “26 pay periods”.
- Clear outputs rather than vague marketing language.
- “Free” when the tool is actually free.
- Explanatory content, formulas, examples and FAQ sections around the calculator.

WorkPay Tools should compete on clarity and task coverage rather than exaggerated promises.
