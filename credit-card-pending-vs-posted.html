Credit Card Pending vs Posted: Why the Status Matters

Understand the difference between pending and posted card transactions and why available credit and statement balances can change.

The short answer

A pending card transaction has been authorized or is being processed but has not yet fully posted to the account. A posted transaction is recorded in the account’s settled activity. The timing depends on the merchant, issuer, weekends, returns and the type of transaction.

What to check

Pending activity can reduce available credit even before it appears in the statement balance. A merchant may also adjust or release an authorization. Do not assume a pending amount is final until it posts, and do not ignore a transaction merely because it is still pending.

Use the right information

Keep receipts and review account activity. If a payment or charge does not appear correctly, follow the issuer’s billing-error instructions and continue paying undisputed amounts as required.

Official reference

The CFPB directs consumers to review statements and follow the billing-error process when a payment is missing or incorrect. Read the source.

Frequently asked questions

What should I use for an account-specific answer?

Use the current account agreement, statement or official plan document. General terms do not override those records.
