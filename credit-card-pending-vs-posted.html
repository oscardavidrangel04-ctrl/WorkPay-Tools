Pending vs Posted Credit Card Payment: What It Means

Learn what pending vs posted means for a credit card payment, how posting differs from the effective credit date, and what to check near a due date.

The short answer

A pending credit card payment has been submitted or is still being processed; a posted payment appears in the issuer’s account activity. The date it appears on screen may differ from the date the issuer treats it as received or credited. If the due date is close, check the issuer’s payment history, cutoff and time zone, and save the confirmation.

Pending payment vs. pending purchase

ActivityWhat pending can meanWhat to verify
Card purchaseThe merchant has requested authorization, but the final transaction has not posted.Check the final amount after the merchant completes or releases the authorization.
Payment to the card issuerThe payment was submitted or is still processing; the account activity may not show it as posted yet.Save the confirmation and check the issuer’s effective received or credited date, cutoff and time zone.

A pending label is not by itself proof that a due-date payment was late or on time. The CFPB explains when a card payment is generally considered received. For a payment close to its due date, follow the issuer’s instructions and retain the timestamp or confirmation.

What to check

Pending activity can reduce available credit even before it appears in the statement balance. A merchant may also adjust or release an authorization. Do not assume a pending amount is final until it posts, and do not ignore a transaction merely because it is still pending.

Use the right information

Keep receipts and review account activity. If a payment or charge does not appear correctly, follow the issuer’s billing-error instructions and continue paying undisputed amounts as required.

Official reference

The CFPB directs consumers to review statements and follow the billing-error process when a payment is missing or incorrect. Read the source.

Frequently asked questions

Does a pending credit card payment count as on time?

A pending label alone does not establish the effective date. Check the issuer’s received or credited date, applicable cutoff and time zone, and keep the confirmation.

Can a pending card transaction reduce available credit?

Yes. An authorization hold can reduce the displayed available credit before the transaction posts; the final amount or hold period can change.

How long does a pending credit card payment take to post?

There is no universal posting time. It depends on the issuer and payment method. Check the payment history for its effective credit date and contact the issuer if the status remains unresolved.

What should I do if a payment does not appear?

Save the payment confirmation and contact the issuer using the number on your card or statement. Follow its instructions and continue meeting any undisputed payment obligations.
