What Is Credit Monitoring? Alerts and Limits

Credit monitoring may alert a consumer when it detects certain changes in credit-file information.

The short answer

An alert can prompt a review, but it does not replace checking reports, securing accounts or resolving an error through the right process.

What to review

Check covered bureaus, alert types and timing, then review the underlying account or report.

What credit monitoring can and cannot do

Credit monitoring can alert you to selected changes in a credit file, such as a new inquiry or account update. It does not prevent identity theft, guarantee that every change is detected immediately or repair inaccurate information for you.

Related questions

Does credit monitoring cover all three bureaus?

Only if the service states that it monitors all three. Some services monitor one bureau.

Is credit monitoring the same as a credit freeze?

No. Monitoring sends alerts; a freeze restricts access to a credit file for many new-credit decisions.

Monitoring versus prevention

An alert about a file change does not itself block a new account. Compare a credit freeze and fraud alert before choosing an identity-protection step.
