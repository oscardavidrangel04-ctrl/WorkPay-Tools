Can Debt Collectors Call You at Work?

Learn when a debt collector may call at work, limits on contacting your employer, and how to ask that workplace calls stop.

The short answer

A collector may contact you about a debt, but federal rules limit inconvenient workplace contact and what the collector may tell other people.

Tell the collector when work calls are not allowed

If your employer prohibits personal calls or work is an inconvenient place to talk, tell the collector clearly. Keep a dated record of the call or message. The CFPB says a collector aware that you cannot receive these calls at work must not contact you there.

Can a collector speak to your employer?

A collector may in limited circumstances seek location information, such as where you work or how to reach you. Generally, the collector may not tell your employer that you owe a debt. Ask a person who took the call what was said and keep the details.

If the calls continue

Document dates, numbers, messages and your request. Review the CFPB guidance and consider submitting a complaint or seeking legal assistance if the collector disregards the restriction. A request about work calls does not itself erase a valid debt.
