Debt and credit planning

What Is Debt Settlement?

Debt settlement is an effort to resolve a debt for less than the amount claimed as owed, often through a negotiated arrangement.

The short answer

Review fees, creditor participation, credit-report effects, potential collection activity and any tax-related notice with qualified help when needed.

What to check

Compare the current statement, agreement or offer before acting. Focus on the amount owed, APR, fees, required payment, due date and any promotional or service terms that apply to your specific accounts.
