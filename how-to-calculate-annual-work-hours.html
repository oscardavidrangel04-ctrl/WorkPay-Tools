How to Calculate Annual Work Hours

Estimate annual work hours using weekly hours and working weeks.

The basic method

A planning estimate multiplies expected weekly hours by working weeks. Paid leave, unpaid leave, holidays, overtime and variable schedules can change actual annual hours.

What to verify

Use the written policy, statement, contract or plan document for the real terms. A simple formula cannot account for every timing rule, fee, benefit or eligibility condition.

Planning example

Multiply 40 weekly hours by 50 working weeks for a 2,000-hour estimate. Subtract expected unpaid time when comparing a contract schedule or annual income assumption.

Question to check

Are paid holidays work hours?

They can be paid but not worked; decide which definition matches your calculation.
