How to Calculate Annual Work Hours

Estimate annual work hours using weekly hours and working weeks.

The basic method

A planning estimate multiplies expected weekly hours by working weeks. Paid leave, unpaid leave, holidays, overtime and variable schedules can change actual annual hours.

Separate paid hours from hours worked

For a 40-hour weekly schedule, 40 × 52 gives 2,080 scheduled annual hours. If two of those weeks are leave with no work, subtract 80 hours to get 2,000 hours actually worked. Paid leave may still be part of paid hours even though it is not time worked.

Avoid subtracting leave twice

If you already multiply 40 hours by 50 working weeks, the result already excludes those two weeks. Do not subtract the same 80 hours again.

  1. Choose whether you need paid hours, scheduled hours or actual work hours.
  2. Use a full-year calendar or add recorded hours for each week.
  3. Separate overtime, unpaid absences and leave according to that purpose.

For a changing schedule, adding weekly records is more reliable than assuming every week is identical. This estimate does not determine overtime eligibility.

Planning example

Question to check

Are paid holidays work hours?
