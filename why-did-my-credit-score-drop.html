Why Did My Credit Score Drop? Common Reasons

A credit score can change when report information changes or a model receives a different account snapshot.

The short answer

Reported balances, payment history, new accounts, inquiries and corrected report information can affect a score.

What to review

Compare report dates, balances, payment status, account openings, limit changes and inquiries. Different services can use different models or update dates.
