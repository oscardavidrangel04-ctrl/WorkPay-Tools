Debt Relief vs Debt Consolidation

Debt relief is a broad term that can include several services; consolidation generally refers to combining or restructuring balances.

The short answer

Read contracts, fees, payment handling, credit consequences and claims about guaranteed results before sharing account information.

What to check

Compare the current statement, agreement or offer before acting. Focus on the amount owed, APR, fees, required payment, due date and any promotional or service terms that apply to your specific accounts.
