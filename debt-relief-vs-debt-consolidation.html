Debt Relief vs Debt Consolidation

Debt relief is a broad term that can include several services; consolidation generally refers to combining or restructuring balances.

The short answer

Read contracts, fees, payment handling, credit consequences and claims about guaranteed results before sharing account information.

Look beyond the advertisement label

Debt relief is a broad label; consolidation is a specific method of combining debts through replacement borrowing. Ask what action a provider will actually take before comparing its advertised payment with your current bills.

Write down these five answers

  1. Am I applying for a loan or paying for a service?
  2. Which accounts are covered?
  3. Who gets the money and on what dates?
  4. Which amounts are guaranteed by a contract and which are estimates?
  5. What costs or remaining balances are excluded from the advertisement?

A single advertised monthly number is not enough to compare two options. Use a worksheet with separate columns for payment, fees, duration and unpaid accounts. Keep copies of the offer and agreement; if the written terms do not match the explanation, resolve that difference before proceeding.

Reference: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau guidance.
