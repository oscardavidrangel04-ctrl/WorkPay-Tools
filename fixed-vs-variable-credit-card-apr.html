Fixed vs Variable Credit Card APR

Compare fixed vs variable credit card APR, how an index can affect a variable rate and what a fixed rate does not guarantee.

The short answer

A variable credit card APR can change when the index identified in the agreement changes. A fixed APR does not automatically move with an index, but fixed does not mean the issuer can never change it under applicable account terms and notice rules.

How a variable APR works

A variable rate is commonly described as an index plus a margin. Changes to the index can change the APR according to the agreement, subject to any stated floor or other term.

What fixed APR means

Fixed describes the rate structure, not a permanent promise. Review notices and the card agreement for circumstances in which a rate can change.

What to compare before carrying a balance

Compare the current APR, rate structure, introductory period, fees and likely payoff time. A low promotional rate can end while a balance remains.

Official reference

Review current guidance from the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau and the terms of the specific account.

Frequently asked questions

Can a variable APR decrease?

It can move with its stated index, subject to the agreement terms.

Can a fixed APR increase?

It may change under applicable account and notice rules; fixed does not mean permanent.

Where is the index or margin disclosed?

Check the cardholder agreement and rate disclosures.
