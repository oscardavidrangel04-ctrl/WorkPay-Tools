Balance Transfer vs Personal Loan

A balance transfer moves eligible revolving debt to a card; a personal loan creates an installment repayment obligation.

The short answer

Review transfer fees, promotional end dates, purchase APR, loan origination fees and the total repayment schedule.

What to check

Compare the current statement, agreement or offer before acting. Focus on the amount owed, APR, fees, required payment, due date and any promotional or service terms that apply to your specific accounts.
