Employee Stock Options vs RSUs: Basic Comparison

Compare employee stock options and restricted stock units at a high level, including vesting and why award documents matter.

The short answer

Stock options generally give an employee the right to buy company stock at a stated exercise price after conditions are met. Restricted stock units (RSUs) generally represent a promise to deliver shares or cash after vesting conditions are satisfied. The plan, grant agreement and company status determine the real terms.

What to check

Both can have value that changes with company performance and market conditions. Options can have no practical value if the share price is below the exercise price, while RSUs can be taxable or subject to withholding when they vest. This is not investment or tax advice.

Use the right record

Review the award type, number of units, vesting schedule, exercise window, expiration, tax treatment and liquidity restrictions. Do not compare headline grant values without those facts.
