With five time-and-a-half hours

At $50 per hour, the 1.5× overtime rate is $75. A 45-hour week with 40 regular hours and five overtime hours produces $2,375 gross pay.

Unpaid time off scenarios

At 40 hours per week, 50 paid weeks equal $100,000 annually; 48 paid weeks equal $96,000. Paid holidays and PTO may keep annual gross pay closer to the full 52-week estimate.
