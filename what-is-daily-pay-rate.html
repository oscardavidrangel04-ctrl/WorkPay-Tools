Pay-rate terminology

What Is a Daily Pay Rate?

A daily pay rate is a gross-pay amount assigned to one workday under a stated schedule or payroll method. For salary comparisons, it can be estimated by dividing annual salary by modeled paid workdays.

Reviewed September 15, 2026

Daily salary rate formula

Daily salary rate = annual salary ÷ annual paid workdays

A five-day schedule across 52 paid weeks represents 260 paid days. A $65,000 salary divided by 260 equals $250 per paid day.

How to calculate wages per day

Choose a paid-day count that matches the annual amount. A part-year or unpaid-week schedule should not automatically use 260 days. Use the Daily Salary Calculator to enter days per week and paid weeks.

Daily rate, hourly rate and prorated salary

A daily rate compares pay per day. An hourly equivalent divides salary by annual hours. Prorated salary estimates the payable portion of a specific pay period. These figures answer different questions and should not be substituted blindly.

Daily pay rate FAQ

How many workdays are in a salary year?

A common five-day, 52-week model has 260 paid days, but the correct assumption depends on the schedule.

Is a daily salary rate gross pay?

This calculation produces a gross-pay equivalent before taxes and deductions.

Can I multiply daily pay by days worked?

You can model earnings that way when the daily rate and payable-day convention apply to the same calculation.
