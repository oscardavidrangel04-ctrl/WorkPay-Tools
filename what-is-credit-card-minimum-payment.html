What Is a Credit Card Minimum Payment?

Understand what a minimum payment represents and why account terms control.

The basic method

A credit-card minimum payment is the amount required by the issuer to keep an account current for the statement period. The formula, floor, fees and treatment of interest are set by the card agreement.

What to verify

Use the written policy, statement, contract or plan document for the real terms. A simple formula cannot account for every timing rule, fee, benefit or eligibility condition.

Why the statement matters

A minimum payment keeps an account from being overdue when paid on time, but it does not show the fastest or lowest-interest payoff path. The issuer statement shows the actual amount due.

Question to check

Can the minimum payment change?

Yes. It can change with balance, interest, fees, promotional terms and issuer policy.
