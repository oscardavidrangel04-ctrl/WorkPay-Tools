How Can Debt Settlement Affect Credit?

A settled debt can be reported differently from an account paid according to its original terms, and account history can remain relevant.

The short answer

Do not rely on a promised score result. Review your credit report, service agreement and official consumer information before acting.

What to check

Compare the current statement, agreement or offer before acting. Focus on the amount owed, APR, fees, required payment, due date and any promotional or service terms that apply to your specific accounts.
