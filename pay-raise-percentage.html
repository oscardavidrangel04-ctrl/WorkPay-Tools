Questions to check with an offer

  • When does the increase take effect?
  • Does it change bonus or overtime eligibility?
  • Are benefits calculated from base salary?
  • Is the quoted change gross or take-home pay?

Frequently asked questions

What is a 5% raise on $50,000?

It adds $2,500, producing a new annual salary of $52,500.

Is a raise calculated before taxes?

Raise offers and calculators usually compare gross pay before taxes and deductions.

Raise percentage examples by salary

The same percentage produces a different dollar increase at each salary level. A 3% raise adds $1,200 to a $40,000 salary, $1,800 to a $60,000 salary and $3,000 to a $100,000 salary. A 5% raise adds $2,000, $3,000 and $5,000 to those salaries respectively.

Current salary3% raise5% raise10% raise
$40,000$1,200$2,000$4,000
$60,000$1,800$3,000$6,000
$100,000$3,000$5,000$10,000

To find the percentage from two known salaries, use (new salary − old salary) ÷ old salary × 100. Review the complete salary increase formula, then compare the result with inflation before evaluating purchasing power.
