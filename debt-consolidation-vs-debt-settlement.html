Debt Consolidation vs Debt Settlement

Debt consolidation combines or restructures debt; debt settlement is an attempt to resolve debt for less than the full amount owed.

The short answer

Read all fees, loan terms, credit implications and tax-related notices. Do not stop payments or make decisions based only on an advertisement.

What to check

Compare the current statement, agreement or offer before acting. Focus on the amount owed, APR, fees, required payment, due date and any promotional or service terms that apply to your specific accounts.
