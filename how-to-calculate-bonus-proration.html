How to Calculate Bonus Proration

Learn a basic monthly proration method for a stated annual bonus.

The basic method

A simple proration multiplies the full-year bonus by eligible months divided by twelve. Employer plan documents can use different eligibility dates, performance rules and payment terms.

Example: a partial-year bonus target

A $12,000 full-year target prorated for nine eligible months out of twelve gives $12,000 × 9 ÷ 12 = $9,000. This is a gross planning amount before any performance adjustment or payroll withholding, not a guaranteed award.

Months and days are different methods

A plan using eligible days requires eligible days divided by total days in its performance period. Do not replace that rule with whole months just because it is simpler. Join dates, leave and termination dates can affect the eligible period under the written plan.

  1. Identify the full-period target and the period it covers.
  2. Use the plan's eligibility units: days, months or another stated basis.
  3. Apply any separate performance factor only as the plan directs.
  4. Confirm payment timing and approval conditions before treating the estimate as income due.

Separate time proration from the performance factor

Time eligibility and performance are different inputs. Suppose the written plan starts with a $10,000 full-year target, allows six eligible months, and applies an 80% performance factor to that prorated target.

  1. Calculate the time-adjusted target: $10,000 × 6 ÷ 12 = $5,000.
  2. Apply the stated performance factor: $5,000 × 0.80 = $4,000.
  3. Keep that $4,000 gross estimate separate from payroll withholding and take-home pay.

Avoid applying the same reduction twice

If the award notice already shows a prorated $5,000 target, do not multiply it by six-twelfths again. Confirm whether the input is the full-year target, an already adjusted target, or the final approved award.

A plan may use different factors, caps, or approval conditions. This example only shows the arithmetic when the plan uses the stated method. If your target begins as a salary percentage, calculate that full-period target with the annual bonus calculator before applying the time adjustment.

Question to check

Are bonuses always prorated?

No. Some plans use different eligibility rules or are discretionary.
