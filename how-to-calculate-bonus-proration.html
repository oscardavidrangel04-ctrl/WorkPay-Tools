How to Calculate Bonus Proration

Learn a basic monthly proration method for a stated annual bonus.

The basic method

A simple proration multiplies the full-year bonus by eligible months divided by twelve. Employer plan documents can use different eligibility dates, performance rules and payment terms.

What to verify

Use the written policy, statement, contract or plan document for the real terms. A simple formula cannot account for every timing rule, fee, benefit or eligibility condition.

Simple example

If nine of twelve months are eligible, multiply the full-year bonus by 9 divided by 12. Plan documents may define eligibility by dates, active employment or performance conditions instead.

Question to check

Are bonuses always prorated?

No. Some plans use different eligibility rules or are discretionary.
