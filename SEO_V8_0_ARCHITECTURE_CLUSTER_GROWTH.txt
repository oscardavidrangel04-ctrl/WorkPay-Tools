WorkPay Tools V8.0 — Architecture, Cluster & Growth Upgrade

Applied 2026-09-01

Major changes:
1. Rebuilt /calculators as an intent-based hub with five clusters covering all 15 tools.
2. Added a prominent cluster gateway to the home page.
3. Added crawlable cross-links and next-step journeys to every calculator.
4. Added BreadcrumbList schema and visible breadcrumbs without review/rating markup.
5. Expanded About into a methodology/trust hub with math, assumptions, sources and update standards.
6. Added scope/trust notes to calculators so gross-pay estimates and exclusions are clearer.
7. Added unique weekly-hours scenario tables to $15/$20/$25/$30/$35/$40 hourly pages.
8. Added hours-per-week sensitivity tables to $40k/$50k/$60k/$70k/$80k/$100k salary pages.
9. Linked programmatic pages back to core calculator pillars instead of leaving them as isolated pages.
10. Updated sitemap lastmod for current canonical HTML pages; no new mass programmatic URLs were created.

Architecture:
- Pay conversion: Hourly to Salary, Salary to Hourly, Annual Income, Monthly Income
- Pay frequency: Weekly Pay, Biweekly Pay, Semimonthly Pay
- Time & hours: Time Card, Work Hours, Hours to Decimal
- Premium pay: Overtime Pay, Double Time, Shift Differential
- Raises & PTO: Pay Raise, PTO Value

No fabricated ratings, reviews, legal outcomes or tax calculations were added.
