Credit Dispute Letter Template: What to Include

A useful dispute letter identifies the exact report item, explains what is inaccurate and states the requested correction without adding unrelated claims.

Credit dispute letter template

Subject: Credit report dispute

I am writing to dispute the following information on my credit report: [bureau, report number, creditor and account identifier]. The item is inaccurate because [short factual explanation]. I request that you [correct or remove the inaccurate information]. Enclosed are copies of [supporting records]. Please send the investigation result to [mailing address].

Sincerely,
[Name]
[Date]

Keep the request specific

Identify each disputed field separately: balance, status, ownership, dates or payment history. A truthful, itemized dispute is easier to investigate than a generic demand to delete accurate information.

Track the dispute

Keep the submitted letter, attachments, delivery record and response together. Use the bureau current instructions because address and identity-verification requirements can change.

Frequently asked questions

Can one letter dispute several items?

It can, but each item should be identified separately with a factual explanation and supporting record.

Should a dispute letter ask to remove accurate information?

No. A credit-report dispute is intended to address information believed to be inaccurate or incomplete.

Is this template legal advice?

No. It is an organizational example and should be adapted to accurate facts and current official instructions.
