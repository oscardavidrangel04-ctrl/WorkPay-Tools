How to Calculate PTO Accrual

Learn the simple PTO-accrual formula and which policy details to verify.

The basic method

PTO accrual is commonly described as hours earned per pay period, hours worked, or service period. The written employer policy controls eligibility, caps and carryover.

What to verify

Use the written policy, statement, contract or plan document for the real terms. A simple formula cannot account for every timing rule, fee, benefit or eligibility condition.

Example and policy checks

For an hourly accrual system, multiply hours worked by the policy accrual rate. Then check whether the policy has a waiting period, annual cap, carryover rule or separate sick-time bank.

Question to check

Which record controls?

The current written PTO policy and payroll record control, not a generic accrual formula.
