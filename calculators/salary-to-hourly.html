How this is calculated Annual salary ÷ annual work hours = hourly equivalent.

Planning estimate only. It does not determine legal entitlement and may differ from payroll records.

Formula for annual salary to hourly pay

Hourly equivalent = annual salary ÷ (hours per week × working weeks per year).

Worked example

For example, $60,000 ÷ (40 hours × 52 weeks) is about $28.85 per hour.

When to convert annual salary to an hourly wage

Use this page when your starting number is an annual salary. If your starting number is an hourly wage, use the Hourly to Salary calculator instead.

What your hourly equivalent means

The hourly result spreads annual salary across the paid hours implied by your schedule. It is useful for comparison, but it does not turn a salaried job into an hourly job or determine overtime status.

How to calculate an hourly rate from annual salary

  1. Enter your annual salary.
  2. Choose the number of hours you work each week.
  3. Enter the number of working weeks per year.
  4. Multiply weekly hours by working weeks to get annual work hours.
  5. Divide annual salary by annual work hours to calculate your hourly rate.

For a full-time schedule of 40 hours per week and 52 weeks per year, the year contains 2,080 hours. A $50,000 annual salary divided by 2,080 hours is about $24.04 per hour.

What this calculator assumes

  • This calculator treats annual salary as gross pay.
  • The entered weekly hours represent your typical workload.
  • Working weeks represent the schedule used for the comparison.
  • Benefits, bonuses and legally defined regular-rate concepts are outside this simple conversion.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Assuming the hourly equivalent is necessarily a legal payroll regular rate.
  • Ignoring large differences in expected weekly hours between jobs.
  • Comparing salary and hourly jobs without considering paid time off or benefits.

How much is a yearly salary per hour?

For a standard 40-hour week and 52 working weeks, divide annual salary by 2,080 hours. For example, $45,000 a year is about $21.63 per hour, $65,000 is about $31.25 per hour, and $70,000 is about $33.65 per hour. Use your real weekly hours and working weeks above when your schedule differs.

Contract hourly equivalent vs. effective hourly rate

The standard salary-to-hourly conversion divides annualized salary by the weekly hours and working weeks you enter. That gives a useful contract-style hourly equivalent.

If your actual workweek is longer than the schedule used for the conversion, the effective hourly rate is lower because the same salary is spread across more hours. This can be useful when comparing a salaried offer with an hourly job.

For example, a salary that looks strong at 40 hours per week may have a noticeably different hourly value if the real schedule averages 45 or 50 hours.

Salary and hourly pay FAQ

How do I convert salary to hourly pay?

Divide your annual salary by the total number of hours you work in a year. Multiply weekly hours by working weeks to find annual work hours.

What weekly hours should I use?

Use the number of hours you usually work each week. Do not use 40 hours if your normal schedule is different.

Does the result include benefits?

No. This calculator compares gross cash pay only.

Is the result my legal regular rate for overtime?

Not necessarily. Legal regular-rate calculations can follow specific rules that this comparison tool does not determine.

Can I use fewer than 52 working weeks?

Yes. Enter the number of weeks you expect to work during the year.
