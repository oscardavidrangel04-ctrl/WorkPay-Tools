How this calculator works

Estimate the hourly equivalent of an annual salary using your expected weekly hours and working weeks. It is useful for comparing salaried and hourly jobs on the same basis.

Hourly equivalent = annual salary ÷ (hours per week × working weeks per year).

Example

A $60,000 salary over 40 hours per week and 52 weeks is about $28.85 per hour, with a monthly average of $5,000.

How to use it

  1. Enter annual gross salary.
  2. Enter expected hours worked per week.
  3. Enter working weeks per year.
  4. Review the hourly equivalent and pay averages.

Frequently asked questions

Is the hourly equivalent my legal regular rate?

Not necessarily. This calculator is a simple comparison tool; legal regular-rate calculations can follow different rules.

Does it include benefits?

No. It compares cash salary only and does not assign a value to insurance, retirement contributions, bonuses or other benefits.

Can I use fewer than 52 weeks?

Yes. Use the working-weeks field to model your actual schedule.
