Formula and worked example

Raise amount = current salary × raise percentage. New salary = current salary + raise amount.

Worked example

Example: a 5% raise on $60,000 adds $3,000 per year, producing a new annual salary of $63,000.

What this calculator assumes

  • The raise is applied to annual gross salary.
  • Monthly increase is annual increase divided by 12.
  • Biweekly increase is annual increase divided by 26.
  • Taxes, deductions and benefit changes are excluded.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Confusing a 5% raise with adding five percentage points to a tax rate.
  • Comparing gross raise amount with take-home increase.
  • Assuming a raise percentage automatically reflects inflation.

Pay Raise Calculator FAQ

How do I calculate a percentage pay raise?

Multiply current salary by the raise percentage, then add that raise amount to current salary.

What is a 5% raise on $60,000?

It is $3,000 per year, making the new annual salary $63,000.

Does the calculator show take-home pay?

No. It shows gross pay changes.

Can I enter a decimal raise such as 3.5%?

Yes.

Does this adjust for inflation?

No. It measures the nominal pay change only.
