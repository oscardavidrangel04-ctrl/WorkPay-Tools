How this calculator works

See how a percentage raise changes annual salary and how much additional gross income that represents per month and per biweekly paycheck.

Raise amount = current salary × raise percentage. New salary = current salary + raise amount.

Example

A 5% raise on a $60,000 salary adds $3,000 per year, creating a new annual salary of $63,000.

How to use it

  1. Enter current annual salary.
  2. Enter raise percentage.
  3. Review your new annual salary.
  4. Compare annual, monthly and biweekly increases.

Frequently asked questions

Does this show take-home increase?

No. It shows gross increases before taxes and deductions.

Can I enter a decimal raise?

Yes. For example, 3.5% is supported.

Does it account for inflation?

No. It measures nominal pay only.
