How this calculator works

Convert annual salary into a 26-paycheck estimate, or use hourly pay and weekly hours to estimate a two-week gross amount.

Salary method: biweekly pay = annual salary ÷ 26. Hourly method: biweekly pay = hourly rate × weekly hours × 2.

Example

A $65,000 annual salary divided across 26 pay periods is $2,500 gross per biweekly paycheck.

How to use it

  1. Choose salary or hourly mode.
  2. Enter annual salary, or hourly rate and weekly hours.
  3. Review estimated biweekly gross pay.
  4. Use the annual total to check the conversion.

Frequently asked questions

Why 26 paychecks?

A biweekly schedule pays every two weeks, which normally produces 26 pay periods in a 52-week year.

Is twice a month the same as biweekly?

No. Semimonthly pay normally means 24 pay periods per year, while biweekly pay normally means 26.

Does hourly mode include overtime?

No. It is a straight-rate estimate. Use the Weekly Pay or Overtime calculator when overtime applies.
