Formula and worked example

Salary mode: biweekly pay = annual salary ÷ 26. Hourly mode: biweekly pay = hourly rate × weekly hours × 2.

Worked example

Example: $65,000 annual salary ÷ 26 pay periods = $2,500 gross per biweekly paycheck.

What this calculator assumes

  • Biweekly means every two weeks and is modeled as 26 pay periods per year.
  • Hourly mode uses one straight hourly rate.
  • Hourly overtime is not included in this tool.
  • Monthly output is an annual average divided by 12.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Confusing biweekly pay with semimonthly pay.
  • Assuming every calendar month contains exactly two biweekly paychecks.
  • Using hourly mode when overtime materially changes the paycheck.

Biweekly Pay Calculator FAQ

How many biweekly pay periods are in a year?

A standard 52-week year divided into two-week periods produces 26 pay periods.

Is biweekly the same as twice a month?

No. Semimonthly pay typically means 24 pay periods per year, while biweekly typically means 26.

How do I calculate biweekly pay from salary?

Divide annual salary by 26 for a simple gross-pay estimate.

Does hourly mode include overtime?

No. Use the Weekly Pay or Overtime Pay calculator when overtime applies.

Is the result before taxes?

Yes. It is estimated gross pay.
