How to calculate an annual bonus

Multiply eligible annual base salary by the bonus percentage divided by 100. A $100,000 salary with a 10% bonus produces $10,000. At 15%, the same salary produces $15,000. This is the percentage-based target, not a prediction of the employer’s final award.

Target bonus vs actual payout

A plan may adjust the target for company results, individual performance or eligible service. This calculator has no performance multiplier and does not guarantee a payout. Use the prorated bonus calculator if the plan uses eligible months. A flat-dollar award does not need a salary-percentage conversion.

Calculator FAQ

Is this bonus after tax?

No. The result is the gross percentage-based bonus before withholding or deductions.

Do I enter base salary or total compensation?

Enter the eligible salary base specified in the bonus plan, not salary plus benefits unless the plan expressly uses that amount.

Does this include partial-year proration?

No. It models a full-period percentage award; use the bonus proration tool for an eligible-month method.

Can a bonus percentage exceed 100%?

Yes mathematically. Use the rate from the applicable plan; this tool does not decide whether it is realistic.
