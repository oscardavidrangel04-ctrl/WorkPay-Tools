Frequently asked questions

Does this subtract my existing emergency savings?

No. It calculates the full target from monthly essential expenses and the number of months. Subtract savings already assigned to the emergency fund separately.

Does it tell me how many months I should choose?

No. You select the scenario. It does not assess your employment stability, insurance, dependents or individual risk.

Emergency fund target examples

At $3,500 in essential monthly expenses, three months totals $10,500 and six months totals $21,000. These are scenarios you choose, not a personalized recommendation. The calculator uses expenses rather than salary because it estimates spending coverage.

Build the monthly expense input

Include housing, basic food, utilities, insurance, transportation and required debt payments. For essential bills paid annually, divide the annual amount by 12. Keep optional spending separate unless you want it included in your scenario.

Target amount versus savings still needed

If your target is $10,500 and you already have $4,000 set aside, the remaining gap is $6,500. This calculator shows the full target; it does not subtract your existing savings. Use the monthly savings goal calculator to turn the gap into a contribution plan.

Planned bills need a different bucket

A known annual insurance bill is not an unexpected event. Compare sinking funds and emergency funds to avoid counting the same savings twice.
