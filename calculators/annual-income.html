Formula and worked example

Annual gross income = hourly rate × average weekly hours × paid weeks per year.

Worked example

Example: $30/hour × 35 hours/week × 50 paid weeks = $52,500 estimated annual gross income.

What this calculator assumes

  • Weekly hours are treated as an average.
  • The same hourly rate applies throughout the model.
  • Paid weeks determine how much of the year is included.
  • Bonuses, overtime and deductions are excluded.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Automatically using 52 weeks despite unpaid gaps.
  • Using gross annual income as a take-home estimate.
  • Ignoring overtime if it is a regular part of earnings.

Annual Income Calculator FAQ

How do I calculate annual income from hourly pay?

Multiply hourly rate by average weekly hours and paid weeks per year.

What should I enter for paid weeks?

Use the number of weeks for which the entered weekly schedule and wage are expected to apply.

Does annual income include overtime?

Not in this simple conversion.

Does it include bonuses?

No. Add bonuses separately if you are comparing total cash compensation.

Is annual income before taxes?

Yes. The result is gross.
