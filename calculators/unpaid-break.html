Formula

Shift hours − (break minutes ÷ 60)

This calculator uses the inputs above to provide a transparent planning estimate. Change the assumptions to compare scenarios.

How to use the result

Use the result as one input to a pay, benefit, credit or budget decision. Written employer, lender and account terms control the actual amount.

Subtract break minutes from shift hours

Convert unpaid minutes to hours before subtraction. An 8.5-hour shift minus a 30-minute break equals 8 paid hours. An 8-hour shift minus 45 minutes equals 7.25 hours, not 7 hours 25 minutes. Enter the total of breaks that are actually unpaid.

Time calculation does not determine break eligibility

The tool subtracts the amount entered; it does not decide whether a break legally qualifies as unpaid. Do not subtract a paid break. Confirm applicable requirements and employer time records. For start and end times, use the time card calculator; for a weekly total, use work hours.

Calculator FAQ

Why does the result use decimal hours?

Decimal hours work directly with hourly pay. For example, 7.5 hours means 7 hours and 30 minutes.

Can the break exceed the shift?

No. Recheck the units or duration if unpaid minutes exceed the shift length.

Does this decide whether lunch must be unpaid?

No. It only performs arithmetic with your entered unpaid duration.

Can I subtract several breaks?

Yes. Add their unpaid minutes together, without including paid breaks.
