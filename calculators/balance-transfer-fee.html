Balance transfer costs

Balance Transfer Fee Calculator

Estimate a one-time balance transfer fee when an offer charges a percentage of the amount moved or a stated minimum, whichever is greater. This does not estimate interest or approval.

Enter the offer terms

ESTIMATED TRANSFER FEE—

Check whether your offer uses a minimum, a flat fee, a cap, or another rule. A 0% APR does not necessarily mean no transfer fee.

Formula used

Fee = greater of (amount × fee percentage) or minimum fee

The total placed on the new card is modeled as the transfer amount plus the fee. Some offers use a different rule, so the agreement overrides this estimate.

Example

A $5,000 transfer at 3% with a $5 minimum produces a $150 fee and a modeled new balance of $5,150. The calculator does not assume the new card has enough available credit for both the transfer and fee.

Fees are not interest savings

A lower promotional APR may reduce interest, but the transfer fee is still a cost. Compare the fee, promotional duration, post-promotion APR and expected payoff time before deciding whether a transfer is useful.

Official source: CFPB balance transfer fee guidance.

Balance Transfer Fee FAQ

Can a 0% balance transfer have a fee?

Yes. Promotional APR and transfer fee are separate terms.

Does the fee become part of the new balance?

It often does, but check how your issuer posts the fee and how it affects your credit limit.

Does this calculate interest savings?

No. It isolates the upfront transfer fee; interest depends on rates, timing and payments.

When a minimum balance transfer fee changes the result

A 3% fee on a $100 transfer is $3. If the offer instead charges the greater of 3% or $5, the fee is $5 and the new balance is $105 when the fee is financed. On a $5,000 transfer, the percentage fee is $150. Confirm how your offer collects the fee.

Compare cost separately from the promotional rate

A zero-percent introductory APR does not make a transfer free. Use the interest savings comparison separately, then account for this upfront cost. Neither calculation establishes approval or available transfer capacity.
