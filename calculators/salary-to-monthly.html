How to use the result

The result is an average gross monthly amount, not a paycheck schedule. A $72,000 annual salary averages $6,000 per month. If paid every two weeks, divide the annual amount by the actual number of pay periods to estimate each check; months with two or three deposits will not match the same monthly average.

Convert annual salary to monthly gross income

Divide the annual salary by 12. A $72,000 salary gives $6,000 per month; $60,000 gives $5,000. This is a monthly average of fixed annual base pay, before payroll deductions. Do not add a discretionary bonus unless you intend to include it in the annual amount.

Monthly income is not always one paycheck

A semimonthly schedule commonly divides annual pay into 24 periods; biweekly commonly uses 26. Two biweekly checks are not the same as one twelfth of annual salary. Use salary per pay period for a specific payroll frequency. For an hourly schedule with variable hours, use hourly monthly income instead.

Calculator FAQ

Is $72,000 a year $6,000 a month?

Yes as gross monthly average: $72,000 divided by 12.

Is this take-home pay?

No. Taxes, insurance and retirement deductions are not subtracted.

Should I use 4 weeks to convert annual salary?

No. Divide fixed annual salary by 12; multiplying weekly pay by four understates a full-year monthly average.

Does unpaid leave change the amount?

It can change actual earnings. This tool assumes the annual amount you enter is earned over the year.
