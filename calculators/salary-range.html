Salary Range Calculator

Estimate a minimum and maximum salary from a midpoint and a range spread. This is a transparent planning model, not a market benchmark or a compensation-policy recommendation.

Calculate a salary range

ESTIMATED RANGE—

Planning estimate only. It does not determine pay equity, market value or legal compliance.

Formula

Minimum = midpoint × (1 − spread ÷ 2) · Maximum = midpoint × (1 + spread ÷ 2)

With a $75,000 midpoint and 20% spread, the modeled range is $67,500 to $82,500.

What this result means

The spread is centered evenly around the midpoint for simplicity. Real pay ranges can be asymmetric and may reflect job scope, location, performance, experience and employer policy.

Salary Range FAQ

What is a compa-ratio?

Compa-ratio is employee salary divided by midpoint. It is a comparison measure, not a recommendation.

Can I use this for a job offer?

Use it to organize assumptions, then compare the complete offer with base pay, variable compensation and benefits.

Check how your policy defines range spread

This calculator defines spread as range width divided by midpoint. At a $75,000 midpoint and 20%, width is $15,000, producing $67,500 to $82,500. A policy that defines spread as (maximum − minimum) ÷ minimum would call that same range 22.22%.

Do not interchange the two percentages

Confirm the denominator before comparing the result with a pay grade. The calculator uses a symmetric midpoint model, not a universal compensation convention. When the endpoints are already known, use the salary midpoint calculator rather than reconstructing the range with an incompatible spread definition.
