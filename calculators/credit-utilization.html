Use posted balances and limits from the relevant date. Pending activity, statement timing and issuer reporting can produce a different snapshot.

How to calculate your credit utilization ratio

Utilization = balance ÷ credit limit × 100

For a combined ratio, enter the sum of balances and the sum of limits. Do not add a credit balance owed by an issuer as if it were borrowing capacity.

Example

A $300 balance on a $1,000 credit limit gives a 30% ratio and $700 in remaining credit. The calculator does not estimate approval odds, interest or a future score.

What this result does not show

A single ratio does not show payment history, account age, reported balances or the exact method used by a lender or score. Review your account terms and credit report for an account-specific question.

Official reference: CFPB credit-use guidance.

Credit Utilization FAQ

Can I use this for multiple cards?

Yes, if you enter combined balances and combined limits. You can also calculate each card separately.

Does a lower ratio guarantee a score increase?

No. The result is only arithmetic; credit scoring and lending decisions use more than one factor.

Why might my number differ from an app?

The balance date, pending transactions, statement status and issuer reporting date may differ.
