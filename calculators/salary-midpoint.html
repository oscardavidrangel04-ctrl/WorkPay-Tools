Salary Midpoint Calculator

Find the arithmetic midpoint between a salary-range minimum and maximum. The midpoint organizes a range but does not establish the correct salary for a person or role.

Calculate salary midpoint

SALARY MIDPOINT—

Salary midpoint formula

Midpoint = (range minimum + range maximum) ÷ 2

A $60,000 minimum and $90,000 maximum have a $75,000 midpoint.

What the midpoint means

The midpoint is the numerical center of the entered range. It is not the average salary paid to employees and does not automatically represent a market rate.

Salary Midpoint FAQ

How do you calculate the midpoint of a salary range?

Add the minimum and maximum, then divide by two.

Is midpoint the same as median?

Not necessarily. The range midpoint is derived from two endpoints; a median requires a set of observations.

Can the midpoint be outside the range?

No. The arithmetic midpoint is exactly halfway between the entered minimum and maximum.
