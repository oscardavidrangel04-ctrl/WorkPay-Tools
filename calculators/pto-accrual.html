PTO accrual example

At an accrual rate of 0.05 hours per hour worked, an 80-hour pay period produces 4 accrued PTO hours. Policies can set different accrual rates by tenure, job type or pay period.

Question to check

Does this show PTO available today?

No. It estimates newly accrued time only; approved leave, caps and prior balances are separate policy items.
