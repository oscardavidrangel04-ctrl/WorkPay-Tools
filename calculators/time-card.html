How this calculator works

Turn a clock-in time, clock-out time and unpaid break into net hours worked. Add an hourly rate to estimate gross pay for that shift.

Net hours = elapsed shift time − unpaid break time. Estimated pay = net hours × hourly rate.

Example

From 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM with a 30-minute unpaid meal break equals 8.0 net hours. At $20/hour, estimated gross pay is $160.

How to use it

  1. Enter clock-in and clock-out times.
  2. Enter any unpaid break in minutes.
  3. Optionally add your hourly rate.
  4. Review elapsed time, unpaid break, net hours and estimated pay.

Frequently asked questions

Does the calculator decide whether a break is paid?

No. Enter only the break time you want deducted. Whether time is compensable depends on the facts and applicable law.

Can it handle an overnight shift?

Yes. If clock-out is earlier than clock-in, the calculator treats clock-out as occurring the next day.

Does it calculate weekly overtime?

No. This page calculates one shift. Use the Work Hours or Overtime calculator for weekly totals.
