Salary Range Penetration Calculator

Calculate where a salary falls between the minimum and maximum of a pay range. This is a mathematical comparison, not a pay recommendation or equity determination.

Calculate range penetration

RANGE PENETRATION—

Salary range penetration formula

(Salary − minimum) ÷ (maximum − minimum) × 100

For a $72,000 salary in a $60,000–$90,000 range, penetration is 40%.

How to interpret the result

0% equals the range minimum, 50% is halfway between minimum and maximum, and 100% equals the maximum. Results below 0% or above 100% indicate salary falls outside the entered range.

Range penetration vs compa-ratio

Range penetration uses both endpoints. Compa-ratio compares salary directly with the midpoint. Do not treat the two percentages as interchangeable.

Salary Range Penetration FAQ

How do you calculate salary range penetration?

Subtract minimum from salary, divide by maximum minus minimum, then multiply by 100.

Can range penetration be over 100%?

Yes. That means salary is above the entered maximum.

Does 50% always equal the published midpoint?

It equals the arithmetic midpoint between the entered minimum and maximum.
