Frequently asked questions

Can I calculate a loan with zero interest?

Yes. With a positive term and a zero rate, the payment equals principal divided by the number of months.

Does the payment include loan fees or insurance?

No. The model assumes a constant rate and equal monthly payments, without separately modeled fees, insurance, taxes or balloon payments.

Fixed monthly loan payment example

A $20,000 loan at an entered annual rate of 8% over 60 months has a modeled payment of about $405.53. At 0%, the payment is simply principal divided by months. The calculation assumes equal monthly payments and a constant rate.

APR and contract interest rate can differ

The rate field is divided by 12 to model monthly interest. A disclosed APR that incorporates loan fees may differ from the contract rate used to calculate installments. This tool does not separately model origination fees, prepaid finance charges or an amount financed different from the entered principal.

A longer term changes more than the monthly payment

With the same positive rate and principal, extending the term usually lowers the payment but increases total modeled interest. Compare both the monthly amount and payment multiplied by the number of months. Insurance, taxes, variable rates, balloon payments and extra repayments are not modeled.
