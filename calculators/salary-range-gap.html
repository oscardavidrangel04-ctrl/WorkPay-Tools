How to read the gap

A negative result means the entered salary is above the midpoint; a positive result means it is below. A midpoint comparison is not a finding of underpayment because role scope and range policy matter.

Calculate the dollar gap to salary midpoint

Subtract current salary from the entered midpoint. With $70,000 salary and $80,000 midpoint, the gap is $10,000. With $85,000 salary, it is −$5,000. Zero means salary equals the midpoint. Use the same currency and annual, monthly or hourly basis for both amounts.

Dollar gap vs compa-ratio and range position

This page answers “how many dollars to the midpoint?” The compa-ratio calculator expresses salary relative to midpoint as a ratio. Range penetration measures location between minimum and maximum. A dollar gap alone does not establish fair pay or the size of an appropriate raise.

Calculator FAQ

Why is the gap negative?

Your entered salary exceeds the midpoint by the absolute value of the result.

Does the gap measure distance to the minimum?

No. This tool compares salary with the midpoint you enter.

How do I find the midpoint?

Use the employer’s stated midpoint or the salary midpoint calculator for an arithmetic midpoint from two endpoints.

Does a positive gap mean I am underpaid?

Not necessarily. Range policy, role scope and market evidence must be considered separately.
