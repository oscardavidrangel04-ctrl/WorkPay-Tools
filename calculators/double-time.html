How this calculator works

Calculate the simple dollar value of hours paid at twice a base hourly rate. Federal FLSA rules do not generally require double-time pay, so use this only when a contract, employer policy or applicable rule provides it.

Double-time rate = hourly rate × 2. Double-time pay = double-time hours × double-time rate.

Example

At $24/hour, 6 hours paid at double time equal a $48 double-time rate and $288 gross double-time pay.

How to use it

  1. Enter regular hourly rate.
  2. Enter the number of hours paid at double time.
  3. Review the 2× rate and total double-time gross pay.
  4. Treat the result as a math estimate, not a determination of entitlement.

Frequently asked questions

Does the FLSA require double time?

No. The U.S. Department of Labor states that the FLSA has no general requirement for double-time pay.

Is double time the same as overtime?

No. Federal overtime for many covered, nonexempt employees is generally at least 1.5× the regular rate after 40 hours in a workweek; double time is 2×.

Does this include regular pay for other hours?

No. It isolates only the hours you enter as double-time hours.
