Frequently asked questions

What does a 50% match up to 6% mean?

The employer contributes half of eligible employee contributions, counting employee contributions only up to 6% of eligible salary in this single-tier model.

Does this calculate vesting or a year-end true-up?

No. Vesting, payroll timing, eligibility, true-ups, tiered matching and annual contribution limits require the plan's actual rules.

Calculate a 50% match up to 6% of pay

For $80,000 of eligible annual pay and a 6% employee contribution, a 50% employer match on contributions up to 6% of pay equals $80,000 × 0.06 × 0.50 = $2,400. Enter 50 for the match percentage and 6 for the salary cap. The cap is not the percentage the employer contributes.

Contributing below or above the cap

At a 4% contribution, that example produces a $1,600 match. At 8%, the modeled match stays $2,400 because only the first 6% is matched. This tool assumes one flat match tier, not a multistep formula.

Annual match estimates have plan-specific limits

The model assumes the entered salary is eligible compensation. It does not apply service eligibility, annual contribution limits, vesting, payroll-by-payroll matching or a year-end true-up. Uneven contributions can produce a different actual match.
