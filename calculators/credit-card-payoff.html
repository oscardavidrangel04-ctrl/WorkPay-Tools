How this payoff estimate works

If you are comparing a balance transfer, calculate its upfront transfer fee and allow for the time a transfer may take. Neither is included in this fixed-payment model.

This model uses one purchase APR. If your balance includes advances, compare purchase APR vs cash advance APR because one rate may not describe the entire balance.

Each modeled month adds APR ÷ 12 interest and subtracts the entered payment. Actual cards often use daily or average-daily-balance methods, so the statement result can differ.

What to enter in a credit card debt payoff calculator

Use the current balance, the purchase APR that applies to that balance, and the monthly amount you can consistently pay. Do not use the credit limit as the balance, and do not combine a cash-advance APR with a purchase APR as though they were the same rate.

For multiple cards, list each balance and rate first. A general debt payoff plan can help organize the accounts before deciding whether a snowball or avalanche order fits the situation.

Why extra payments can help

If you are paying only the required amount, review minimum payment vs statement balance to understand what remains unpaid.

Paying more than the required amount generally reduces principal faster. The CFPB notes that paying more than the minimum can reduce interest costs and repay the balance sooner.

Official source: CFPB credit card guidance.

Credit Card Payoff FAQ

Does this predict my exact payoff date?

No. It is a fixed-payment planning model without fees, new charges, rate changes or multiple APR categories.

What if payment is lower than monthly interest?

The balance will not decline in this model, so increase the payment or review the account terms.

Does this include deferred-interest offers?

No. Deferred-interest promotions require separate treatment under the card agreement.

Can I use this for several credit cards?

Use it for one balance-and-rate scenario at a time. For several cards, first list the balances, APRs and required payments, then choose a repayment order.
