Compa-Ratio Calculator

Compare an employee’s current salary with the midpoint of a salary range. The result is a planning ratio—not a conclusion about market value, performance or pay equity.

Enter a positive salary and midpoint.

Compa-ratio formula

Compa-ratio = current salary ÷ salary range midpoint

To express the result as a percentage, multiply the ratio by 100. A $72,000 salary divided by a $75,000 midpoint equals 0.96, or 96%.

How to interpret a compa-ratio

ResultSimple interpretation
Below 1.00Salary is below the selected midpoint
1.00Salary equals the selected midpoint
Above 1.00Salary is above the selected midpoint

These descriptions are mathematical only. Employers may use different ranges, geographic adjustments and compensation policies.

Compa-ratio vs position in range

Compa-ratio compares salary with the midpoint. Range penetration compares salary with the full distance between the minimum and maximum. They answer related but different questions.

Compa-Ratio FAQ

How do you calculate compa-ratio?

Divide current salary by the salary-range midpoint and multiply by 100 for a percentage.

What does a compa-ratio of 1.00 mean?

Salary equals the selected range midpoint. It is the same as 100%.

What is a good compa-ratio?

There is no universal good ratio. Interpretation depends on the range design, role, experience, performance, location and employer policy.

Is compa-ratio a pay-equity test?

No. It is one comparison measure and cannot establish whether pay is fair or legally compliant.
