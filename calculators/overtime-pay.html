How this calculator works

Estimate weekly gross pay by separating straight-time hours from overtime hours. The default uses 40 hours and a 1.5× multiplier, but you can change both fields for planning.

Regular pay = regular hours × hourly rate. Overtime pay = overtime hours × hourly rate × overtime multiplier.

Example

At $20/hour for 45 hours with a 40-hour threshold and 1.5× multiplier: regular pay is $800, overtime pay is $150, and estimated weekly gross pay is $950.

How to use it

  1. Enter your regular hourly rate.
  2. Enter total hours worked in the workweek.
  3. Adjust the overtime threshold or multiplier only if you have a reason to use a different assumption.
  4. Review regular pay, overtime pay, overtime rate and total gross pay.

Frequently asked questions

What is the default overtime rate?

For many covered, nonexempt employees under the federal FLSA, overtime is at least 1.5 times the regular rate after 40 hours in a workweek. State rules and exemptions can change the result.

Does this calculator include taxes?

No. It estimates gross pay before taxes, benefits, garnishments and other deductions.

Is every worker entitled to overtime?

No. Coverage and exemptions depend on the worker and job. Use this tool as an estimate, not a legal determination.
