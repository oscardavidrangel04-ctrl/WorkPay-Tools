TOTAL GROSS EARNINGS—

Gross estimate only. Your compensation plan determines eligible sales and adjustments.

Sales commission calculator formula

Commission = eligible sales × (commission rate ÷ 100)

Total gross earnings = base pay + commission

On $25,000 of eligible sales at 5%, commission is $1,250. With $2,000 of base pay for the same period, total gross earnings are $3,250.

Sales and commission calculator assumptions

The result uses one flat commission percentage. Enter sales and base pay from the same period. If your plan uses tiers, profit margins, accelerators, recoverable draws, caps, chargebacks or split credit, calculate those rules separately.

Commission calculator FAQ

How do I calculate commission percentage?

Multiply eligible sales by the percentage divided by 100.

How does a commission rate calculator work?

It multiplies eligible sales by the commission rate. For example, 6% of $40,000 is $2,400.

Is commission part of total compensation?

Commission is cash compensation. A broader package may also include salary, bonus, benefits, retirement contributions and equity.

Does this calculate take-home commission?

No. It reports estimated gross commission before withholding and deductions.
