How to calculate an overtime hourly rate

Multiply the entered regular rate by the multiplier. A $24 rate at 1.5 gives $36 per overtime hour. At 2, it gives $48. Enter 1.5, not 150, for time and a half. The result is a rate per hour, not the total payment for the workweek.

Overtime rate vs overtime earnings

Eight hours at a $36 overtime rate produce $288 in overtime pay. Use overtime pay to combine hours and rates. This tool does not decide who qualifies, which hours count, or what must be included in a legal regular rate. Employer agreements and applicable rules can require more than a simple base-wage calculation.

Calculator FAQ

What is time and a half for $24 an hour?

$36 per hour: $24 multiplied by 1.5.

Is the multiplier a percentage?

No. Enter the factor directly, such as 1.5 or 2.

Does this include my regular pay?

No. It calculates only the hourly rate for the entered multiplier.

Does a calculated rate establish overtime eligibility?

No. It is arithmetic; it does not assess exemptions or applicable labor rules.

Overtime hourly rate versus the extra premium

At a $24 base rate and a 1.5 multiplier, the overtime rate is $36. The extra premium above straight time is $12 per hour. For eight qualifying overtime hours, that is $288 in total overtime earnings, including $96 of additional premium. Do not add $288 to another eight hours of straight-time pay, because the $288 already includes the base portion.
