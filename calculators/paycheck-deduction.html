Subtract voluntary deductions from gross pay

With $2,500 gross pay and $350 in entered deductions, the amount remaining is $2,150 before any taxes or other deductions not entered. Keep all amounts on the same pay-period basis. Entering an annual deduction against a single paycheck gives a misleading result.

Why this is not a take-home pay calculator

This tool does not compute federal, state or payroll taxes, and it does not classify a deduction as pre-tax or after-tax. Those distinctions can affect a real payslip. Use the actual pay stub to reconcile all withheld amounts. Read paycheck deductions and gross vs net pay for the broader comparison.

Calculator FAQ

Is the output my net paycheck?

No. It is gross pay less only the voluntary deductions you enter.

Does it calculate tax savings from a deduction?

No. It does not model tax treatment or withholding.

What if deductions exceed gross pay?

The inputs need review; the calculator flags that case rather than calling zero a valid paycheck.

Should both amounts use the same pay period?

Yes. Compare weekly with weekly, biweekly with biweekly, or the applicable matching period.
