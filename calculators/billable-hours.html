Frequently asked questions

Does this calculate an invoice amount?

No. It subtracts non-billable time from total weekly working hours. Revenue and invoice rounding require an agreed rate and contract terms.

What if non-billable time exceeds working time?

The inputs are inconsistent. Correct the totals before using the weekly estimate.

Billable hours formula and example

Forty weekly working hours minus eight non-billable hours leaves 32 billable hours. This is a time estimate, not invoice revenue. Multiply by an agreed rate separately if you need a gross billing estimate.

Include non-billable work consistently

Document administration, marketing, training and other time not billed to clients. Do not subtract a task twice. If non-billable hours exceed total working hours, correct the inputs rather than treating the result as a valid week.

Annualize the realistic billable schedule

Thirty-two billable hours across 46 working weeks is 1,472 annual billable hours. Use that annual figure in the contractor hourly rate calculator, not an automatic 2,080-hour assumption. Contract billing increments and invoice rounding are not modeled here.
