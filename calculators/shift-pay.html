Calculate gross pay for one shift

Add the flat hourly differential to the base rate, then multiply by paid hours. A $22 base rate plus $2 per hour over eight hours gives $192: $176 base pay and $16 differential. Enter paid hours after excluding any genuinely unpaid time.

Flat differential is not a percentage premium

The differential field is dollars per hour, not a percentage. For a 10% premium on $22, first convert it to $2.20 per hour. This calculator applies the same differential to all entered hours. It does not split premium windows, stack multiple differentials or add overtime. Use the unpaid break calculator to calculate entered hours from a known shift duration.

Calculator FAQ

Is the differential applied to every entered hour?

Yes. The tool uses one flat differential for the full paid duration entered.

What if only some hours receive premium pay?

Calculate the eligible hours separately from the other hours, then combine the amounts.

Does this include overtime?

No. It does not determine or add overtime premiums.

Is the output take-home pay?

No. It is gross shift pay before deductions.
