How this calculator works

Estimate an average monthly gross income from an hourly schedule. The calculator annualizes your assumptions and divides the result by 12.

Monthly average = (hourly rate × weekly hours × paid weeks per year) ÷ 12.

Example

At $22/hour, 40 hours per week and 52 paid weeks, estimated average monthly gross income is about $3,813.33.

How to use it

  1. Enter hourly rate.
  2. Enter average weekly hours.
  3. Enter paid weeks per year.
  4. Review weekly, monthly-average and annual estimates.

Frequently asked questions

Why not multiply weekly pay by four?

A year has 52 weeks, so dividing annualized pay by 12 gives a better monthly average than assuming every month has exactly four weeks.

Does this include overtime?

No. It applies one hourly rate to all entered hours.

Is this gross or net income?

Gross. Taxes and other deductions are not subtracted.
