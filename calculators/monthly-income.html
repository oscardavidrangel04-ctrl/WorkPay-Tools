Monthly Income Calculator Formula and Examples

Average monthly gross income = (hourly rate × weekly hours × paid weeks per year) ÷ 12.

Worked example

Example: $22/hour × 40 hours/week × 52 weeks ÷ 12 ≈ $3,813.33 average gross income per month.

Common ways to use this calculator

  • Estimate an average monthly gross income from hourly pay.
  • Budget using annual income divided consistently across 12 months.
  • Compare monthly averages for different hourly rates or schedules.

What this calculator assumes

  • One hourly rate applies to every modeled hour.
  • Weekly hours are treated as an average.
  • Paid weeks can be adjusted for seasonal or unpaid gaps.
  • Overtime and deductions are excluded.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Multiplying weekly pay by four and calling it an exact monthly average.
  • Forgetting unpaid weeks or seasonal gaps.
  • Comparing gross income with take-home income.

How monthly income is estimated

Monthly gross income is easiest to compare when annual income is divided by 12. For hourly workers, annual income depends on hourly rate, hours worked per week and paid weeks per year.

Average monthly income = annual gross income ÷ 12

This is an average monthly figure. Actual paychecks can vary because months contain different numbers of workdays and some pay schedules create uneven numbers of pay dates per month.

Monthly Income Calculator FAQ

How do I calculate monthly income from hourly pay?

Annualize hourly pay using weekly hours and paid weeks, then divide by 12.

Why is monthly income an average?

Weeks do not divide evenly into calendar months, so annual pay divided by 12 is more consistent.

Does this include overtime?

No. It assumes one hourly rate.

Can I model seasonal work?

Yes. Reduce paid weeks per year to match your schedule.

Is this gross income?

Yes. Payroll deductions are not subtracted.
