Frequently asked questions

Does this savings goal calculator include compound interest?

No. It divides the remaining savings gap by a positive number of months, assuming no interest or investment returns.

What if I have already reached my goal?

The required additional monthly contribution is zero when current savings equal or exceed the target.

Calculate monthly savings needed for a goal

For a $6,000 target, $1,000 already saved and 12 months remaining, the required monthly contribution is ($6,000 − $1,000) ÷ 12 = $416.67. The result is rounded for display; slightly rounding deposits upward can help cover the remaining cents.

Compare timelines without assuming investment returns

The same $5,000 gap requires $833.33 per month over six months or $208.33 over 24 months. This calculator assumes no interest or investment growth. It does not project taxes, fees, inflation or changes in the goal's price.

Only count savings assigned to this goal

Do not enter the whole bank balance if some money is reserved for rent or another goal. If savings already equal or exceed the target, the required additional contribution is zero. A future goal needs a positive number of months.
