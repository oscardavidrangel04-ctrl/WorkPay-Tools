Time and a half formula

Overtime rate = regular hourly rate × 1.5

At $20 per hour, time and a half is $30 per hour. Five overtime hours add $150 to $800 of regular weekly pay.

Time and a half versus total overtime pay

The time-and-a-half rate is only the premium hourly rate. Total overtime pay also depends on the number of overtime hours. For schedules with daily entries, thresholds or mixed rates, use the full Overtime Pay Calculator.

Federal overtime context

Under the federal Fair Labor Standards Act, covered nonexempt employees generally receive at least one and one-half times their regular rate for hours over 40 in a workweek. State rules or employment agreements may provide additional protections. See the U.S. Department of Labor.

Frequently asked questions

What does time and a half mean?

It means 1.5 times the regular hourly rate.

What is time and a half for $20 an hour?

The time-and-a-half rate is $30 per hour.

Does this calculator determine overtime eligibility?

No. It calculates the arithmetic after you enter a rate and hours. Eligibility can depend on federal and state rules.

Can I calculate double time?

Use the multiplier field and enter 2.0, or visit the Double Time Calculator.
