Estimate a target salary from a current offer and desired increase.

TARGET SALARY—

Estimate only. It excludes taxes, fees, eligibility and account-specific terms.

Formula

Current offer × (1 + desired increase ÷ 100)

Use a target as a conversation input

An 8% increase on a $75,000 offer produces an $81,000 target estimate. A negotiation can also include title, bonus, equity, location or benefits.

Calculate a salary counteroffer target

Multiply the current offer by one plus the desired increase as a decimal. For $75,000 and 8%, the target is $81,000: a $6,000 annual increase. The calculator applies your chosen percentage; it does not infer the employer’s budget or your market value.

Target, opening ask and minimum acceptable offer

These are different planning numbers. This tool calculates only the target, not an automatic opening anchor or walk-away minimum. Research the role, posted range, responsibilities and total package before choosing your percentage. Compare the compensation package when base salary, bonus and benefits differ. Use pay raise to compare an existing salary with a proposed one.

Calculator FAQ

What is an 8% counteroffer on $75,000?

$81,000 before deductions: $75,000 times 1.08.

Does the tool recommend how much to ask for?

No. You choose the increase; the tool translates it into dollars.

Does it generate a market salary range?

No. It does not use job, location or market compensation data.

Can benefits make a lower base offer more valuable?

They can change the comparison, but value depends on the benefit terms and your circumstances.
