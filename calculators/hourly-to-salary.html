How this calculator works

Convert an hourly wage into a consistent weekly, monthly-average and annual gross-pay estimate. This is useful when comparing an hourly job with a salaried offer.

Annual pay = hourly rate × hours per week × paid weeks per year. Monthly average = annual pay ÷ 12.

Example

At $25/hour, 40 hours per week and 52 paid weeks, estimated annual gross pay is $52,000 and the monthly average is about $4,333.33.

How to use it

  1. Enter your hourly wage.
  2. Enter your usual paid hours per week.
  3. Enter the number of paid weeks in a year.
  4. Compare weekly, monthly-average and annual estimates.

Frequently asked questions

Does hourly to salary include overtime?

No. This conversion assumes the same hourly rate for all entered hours. Use the Overtime Pay Calculator when overtime applies.

Why is monthly pay an average?

A year does not contain exactly four weeks per month, so the calculator divides estimated annual pay by 12.

What if I do not work 52 paid weeks?

Change the paid-weeks field to match your situation, such as unpaid time off or seasonal work.
