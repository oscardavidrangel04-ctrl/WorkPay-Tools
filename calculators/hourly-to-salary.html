Formula and worked example

Annual pay = hourly rate × hours per week × paid weeks per year. Average monthly pay = annual pay ÷ 12.

Worked example

Example: $25/hour × 40 hours/week × 52 paid weeks = $52,000/year, or an average of $4,333.33/month.

What this calculator assumes

  • The same hourly rate applies to every modeled hour.
  • The weekly schedule is reasonably consistent.
  • Paid weeks controls how much of the year is included.
  • Overtime, bonuses and benefits are not included.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Multiplying weekly pay by 4 to estimate a typical month.
  • Using 52 paid weeks when you expect unpaid seasonal gaps.
  • Comparing cash salary without separately considering benefits.

Hourly to Salary Calculator FAQ

How do I convert hourly pay to annual salary?

Multiply hourly rate by paid hours per week, then multiply by paid weeks per year.

Why is the monthly result an average?

A calendar year has 52 weeks, not exactly four weeks per month. Dividing the annual estimate by 12 gives a more consistent monthly average.

Does this calculator include overtime?

No. It applies one hourly rate to the entered hours. Use the Overtime Pay Calculator when overtime applies.

What if I work part time?

Enter your actual average paid hours per week. The formula works for part-time schedules too.

Is this gross or take-home pay?

Gross pay. Taxes and other payroll deductions are not subtracted.
