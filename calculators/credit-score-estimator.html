How the estimator works

The tool combines four self-reported factor categories into a broad illustrative range. It cannot verify report data, model version, bureau or lender criteria.

Use the result correctly

Use the output to identify which report factors to inspect. Do not present it as a score or use it to predict approval, pricing or a future score change.

Frequently asked questions

Is this an official FICO Score calculator?

No. It does not use proprietary FICO or VantageScore models.

Does it pull my credit?

No. Inputs stay in the page and the tool does not access a credit report.
