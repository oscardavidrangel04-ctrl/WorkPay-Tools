How the estimator works

The tool combines four self-reported factor categories into a custom educational factor index. It cannot verify report data, model version, bureau or lender criteria.

Use the result correctly

Use the output to identify which report factors to inspect. Do not present it as a score or use it to predict approval, pricing or a future score change.

What this credit score estimator can actually tell you

Real scores depend on credit-report data and a specific scoring model. This self-reported checklist cannot reproduce those models. Its custom factor index uses 40% payment history, 30% utilization, 20% history length and 10% applications. These are this tool’s illustrative weights, not published FICO or VantageScore weights. No numerical credit-score prediction is provided.

Check your report before interpreting a profile

Start with how to read a credit report. Review reported balances, payment status and account ages. A collection, bankruptcy, thin file or reporting error can matter even when the four selected descriptions look favorable. The index omits those details. For an explanation of real scores, see the CFPB credit-score guide.

Credit factor profile FAQ

Does this predict my FICO Score?

No. It reports an illustrative factor profile, not a FICO Score or a numeric score range.

Why can bureau scores differ?

Bureaus can hold different report data, and scores can use different models or calculation dates.

Does this check affect my credit?

No. The page does not request your report or initiate a credit inquiry.

Can a favorable profile guarantee approval?

No. Lenders consider their own criteria and may use information not included here.
