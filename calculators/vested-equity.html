Planning estimate only. It does not include taxes, legal eligibility rules, fees or terms not entered above.

Formula

Total units × vested percentage × assumed unit price

This calculator uses the inputs above to provide a transparent planning estimate. Change the assumptions to compare scenarios.

How to use the result

Use the result as one input to a pay, benefit, credit or budget decision. Written employer, lender and account terms control the actual amount.

Calculate vested units and assumed gross value

With 1,000 total units and 40% vested, the vested portion is 400 units. At an assumed $12 per unit, their gross illustrative value is $4,800. The percentage must be between 0% and 100%. Use a price appropriate to the same kind of units in the grant.

Vesting value is not cash you can spend

This tool accepts an already-known vested percentage. It does not determine cliffs, vesting dates or forfeiture rules. RSUs, shares and stock options are different instruments. For options, unit price alone does not account for exercise price. Private-company equity may not be sellable at the assumed value. Read the vesting schedule guide and grant agreement before using equity in a budget.

Calculator FAQ

Does this calculate my vesting schedule?

No. Enter the vested percentage from your agreement or administrator records.

Does it deduct taxes?

No. The output is an assumed gross value, not after-tax proceeds.

Can I use this as an option profit calculator?

No. It does not subtract exercise price or model option terms.

Does a vested grant guarantee a sale?

No. Transfer restrictions and market liquidity can prevent or limit a sale.
