Daily Salary Calculator

DAILY SALARY RATE—

Salary to daily rate formula

Daily rate = annual salary ÷ (paid days per week × paid weeks per year)

For $65,000 across 5 days and 52 paid weeks, the daily salary equivalent is $250.

Daily salary rate calculator assumptions

The annual salary and paid-day count must represent the same schedule. Paid leave may already be included in a salary; unpaid weeks reduce the modeled paid days. Do not subtract holidays twice.

Daily pay vs hourly pay

A daily salary equivalent does not show the effective hourly rate unless daily hours are also known. Use the salary to hourly calculator when hours are the relevant comparison.

Daily salary calculator FAQ

How do I calculate salary per day?

Divide annual salary by the paid workdays represented in the year.

What is a salary to daily rate calculator?

It converts an annual salary into a gross amount per modeled paid workday.

Does this calculate take-home daily pay?

No. It does not subtract taxes or deductions.
