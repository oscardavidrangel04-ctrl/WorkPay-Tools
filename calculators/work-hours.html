How this calculator works

Estimate total weekly working time after subtracting an unpaid break from each shift. It is designed for quick schedule planning.

Weekly net hours = (shift hours − break hours) × days worked per week.

Example

Five 8.5-hour shifts with a 30-minute unpaid break each produce 40 net work hours for the week.

How to use it

  1. Enter total scheduled shift length.
  2. Enter unpaid break minutes per shift.
  3. Enter days worked per week.
  4. Review net hours per shift and per week.

Frequently asked questions

Are short breaks always unpaid?

No. Compensability depends on the circumstances and applicable rules. This tool subtracts only the break time you enter.

Can I use decimal shift lengths?

Yes. For example, 8.5 represents eight hours and 30 minutes.

Does 40 hours always mean overtime starts?

Federal overtime rules commonly use over 40 hours for covered, nonexempt employees, but exemptions and state rules can differ.
