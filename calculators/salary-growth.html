PROJECTED SALARY—

Uses annual compounding. Inflation, taxes and deductions are not included.

Salary growth formula

Future salary = current salary × (1 + annual raise rate)years

A $60,000 salary growing 3% annually for five years becomes about $69,556.

Salary Growth FAQ

How does compounding work?

Each annual percentage increase applies to the previous year's salary.

Does this include inflation?

No. The calculator shows nominal salary growth.
