Shift Differential Calculator Formula and Examples

Combined shift rate = base hourly rate + differential per hour. Shift gross pay = combined shift rate × eligible hours.

Worked example

Example: a $20 base rate + $2 differential for 32 eligible hours gives $640 base pay + $64 differential = $704 gross shift pay.

What this calculator assumes

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Adding a differential but ignoring its possible effect on a legal regular-rate calculation.
  • Using this page as an overtime calculator.

Shift Differential Calculator FAQ

What is a shift differential?

It is additional compensation tied to working a particular shift, often expressed as extra dollars per hour or a percentage.

Does federal law require extra pay for night work?

The FLSA does not generally require extra pay simply because work occurs at night or on weekends.

Can a shift differential affect overtime?

Certain compensation can affect the regular rate used for overtime calculations. This simple tool does not determine that legal rate.

Does this calculator include overtime?

No. It values the entered differential for selected hours.

Can I enter a percentage differential?

This version uses a dollar-per-hour differential for clarity.
