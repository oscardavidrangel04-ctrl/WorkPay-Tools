How this calculator works

Estimate the extra gross pay from a shift differential, such as an agreed night-shift premium. The tool uses a simple additional-dollar-per-hour model.

Shift rate = base hourly rate + differential per hour. Shift pay = shift rate × eligible hours.

Example

A $20 base rate plus a $2 night differential for 32 eligible hours produces $704 gross shift pay, including $64 of differential pay.

How to use it

  1. Enter base hourly rate.
  2. Enter the additional differential per eligible hour.
  3. Enter eligible shift hours.
  4. Review base pay, differential pay and combined gross pay.

Frequently asked questions

Does federal law require night-shift premium pay?

The FLSA does not generally require extra pay simply for night or weekend work; such premiums may come from an agreement or other applicable rules.

Can shift differential affect overtime calculations?

It can. Certain compensation may be included when determining the regular rate for overtime. This simple calculator does not determine a legal regular rate.

Does this calculator include overtime?

No. It values the selected shift differential only.
