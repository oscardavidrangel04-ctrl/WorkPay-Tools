Choose the number of annual pay periods

Use 12 for monthly, 24 for semimonthly, 26 for a typical biweekly year, or 52 for a typical weekly year. Confirm the employer’s actual schedule. For $72,000, these produce $6,000, $3,000, about $2,769.23 and about $1,384.62 respectively. The frequency must be a positive whole number.

Biweekly vs semimonthly gross salary

Biweekly means every two weeks; semimonthly means twice a month. They usually produce different check amounts even for the same annual salary. Some payroll calendars have an additional payday; the employer’s annual-salary allocation policy controls. Compare biweekly vs semimonthly pay. This tool does not calculate deductions or prorate partial periods.

Calculator FAQ

How do I calculate salary per paycheck?

Divide annual gross salary by the applicable annual pay-period count.

Can I enter zero pay periods?

No. A positive whole number is required to divide annual salary.

Does 26 always apply to biweekly pay?

It is common, but verify the calendar and employer policy for the relevant year.

Does this estimate net pay?

No. It estimates gross salary per period without deductions.
