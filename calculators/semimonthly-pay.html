ESTIMATED SEMIMONTHLY PAY—

Semimonthly means 24 pay periods per year, typically two per month. Gross-pay estimate only.

Biweekly vs semimonthly pay

Semimonthly pay uses 24 pay periods per year, while biweekly pay commonly uses 26. The checks can look similar, but the schedules are not interchangeable.

Semimonthly pay formula

Semimonthly gross pay = annual gross pay ÷ 24.

For an hourly employee, this tool first estimates annual gross pay from hourly rate × weekly hours × paid weeks, then divides the annual amount by 24.

Semimonthly Pay Calculator FAQ

How many semimonthly paychecks are there?

There are 24 semimonthly pay periods in a standard year.

Is semimonthly the same as biweekly?

No. Biweekly commonly means every two weeks, or 26 pay periods in a standard year.

Is this take-home pay?

No. The result is gross pay before taxes and deductions.

Semimonthly pay: quick answers

How many semimonthly pay periods are in a year?
Semimonthly pay means two pay periods each month, for 24 pay periods per year.
How do I calculate semimonthly pay from salary?
For 24 equal pay periods, divide annual gross salary by 24.
Is semimonthly the same as biweekly?
No. Semimonthly means twice per month; biweekly means every two weeks.
Why is a semimonthly paycheck usually larger than a biweekly paycheck?
The same annual salary is divided into 24 semimonthly checks instead of 26 biweekly checks.
