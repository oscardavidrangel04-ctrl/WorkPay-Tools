How this calculator works

Estimate one week of gross earnings from an hourly wage and hours worked. The calculator separates regular and overtime portions using the assumptions you choose.

Weekly pay = regular hours × hourly rate + overtime hours × hourly rate × overtime multiplier.

Example

At $18/hour for 43 hours with overtime after 40 at 1.5×, estimated weekly gross pay is $801.

How to use it

  1. Enter hourly rate and weekly hours.
  2. Keep or adjust the overtime threshold.
  3. Keep or adjust the overtime multiplier.
  4. Review the regular, overtime and total estimate.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the same as the overtime calculator?

It uses the same core math but is organized around total weekly gross pay.

Does it estimate take-home pay?

No. It estimates gross pay before taxes and deductions.

Can I set a different overtime threshold?

Yes, for planning purposes. Applicable legal rules depend on your situation.
