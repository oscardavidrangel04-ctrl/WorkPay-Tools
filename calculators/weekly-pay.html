Weekly Pay Calculator Formula and Examples

Weekly gross pay = regular pay + overtime pay.

Worked example

Example: $18/hour for 43 hours with overtime after 40 at 1.5× gives $720 regular pay + $81 overtime pay = $801 estimated weekly gross pay.

Common ways to use this calculator

  • Estimate gross weekly earnings from hourly wages.
  • Separate regular pay from overtime pay after a chosen threshold.
  • Compare weekly earnings at different hours or rates.

What this calculator assumes

  • The entered hourly rate is the simple base rate used by this estimator.
  • The selected threshold and multiplier determine the overtime portion.
  • Taxes and payroll deductions are excluded.
  • Complex regular-rate adjustments are excluded.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Confusing gross weekly pay with take-home pay.
  • Using a monthly total of hours instead of one workweek.
  • Assuming a custom threshold changes the law that applies to you.

Weekly Pay Calculator FAQ

What does weekly gross pay mean?

It is pay before taxes, benefits and other payroll deductions.

Does this calculator include overtime?

Yes. Hours above the selected threshold use the selected overtime multiplier.

What overtime defaults are used?

The defaults are 40 hours and 1.5×, reflecting the common federal rule for covered, nonexempt employees.

Can I change the overtime threshold?

Yes, for modeling. The applicable legal rule depends on your circumstances.

Does it include bonuses or commissions?

No. This is a simple hourly-pay estimate.
