ESTIMATED RESULT—

Planning estimate only. It does not determine legal entitlement and may differ from payroll records.

Formula and worked example

Estimated PTO wage value = PTO hours × hourly rate.

Worked example

Example: 80 PTO hours × $25/hour = $2,000 in gross wage value, equal to ten 8-hour workdays.

What this calculator assumes

  • Every PTO hour is valued at the entered hourly rate for this estimate.
  • An 8-hour day is used only for the day-equivalent display.
  • The tool does not determine payout eligibility.
  • Taxes and employer-specific PTO rules are excluded.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Assuming estimated wage value means unused PTO must be paid out.
  • Assuming every employer defines a workday as eight hours.
  • Treating PTO policy questions as purely federal wage-law questions.

PTO Value Calculator FAQ

How do I calculate the value of PTO hours?

Multiply the PTO-hour balance by the hourly rate used for the estimate.

Does this mean my employer must pay unused PTO?

No. Payout requirements can depend on employer policy and applicable state law.

Does federal law require paid vacation?

The FLSA does not generally require pay for time not worked such as vacation.

How are PTO days calculated here?

The display divides PTO hours by eight to show an 8-hour-day equivalent.

Can salaried workers use this calculator?

You can use an estimated hourly equivalent for planning, but actual employer PTO valuation may differ.
