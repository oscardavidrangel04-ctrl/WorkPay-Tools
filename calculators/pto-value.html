How this calculator works

Estimate the gross wage value represented by a PTO balance. This is a planning conversion, not a determination of whether unused PTO must be paid out.

Estimated PTO wage value = PTO hours × hourly rate.

Example

80 PTO hours at $25/hour represent $2,000 in gross wage value and equal ten 8-hour workdays.

How to use it

  1. Enter PTO balance in hours.
  2. Enter hourly wage.
  3. Review estimated gross wage value.
  4. Compare the balance with equivalent 8-hour workdays.

Frequently asked questions

Does this mean my employer must pay unused PTO?

No. This calculator only converts hours to an estimated wage value. Payout rules can depend on employer policy and applicable state law.

Does the FLSA require paid vacation?

The federal FLSA generally does not require payment for time not worked such as vacation; benefits can be governed by agreements and other applicable laws.

Can salaried workers use this?

Yes, if you first estimate an hourly equivalent, but actual PTO policies may value time differently.
