Calculate your share of a commission pool

Multiply the pool by your split as a decimal. A $10,000 commission pool with a 35% share gives $3,500. The remaining 65% is $6,500 before any other arrangement. Enter the commission pool, not the transaction’s sale price.

Commission rate vs commission split

A commission rate calculates a pool from sales. A split distributes that pool among participants. First use the commission calculator if you need to turn sales and a rate into the pool. This tool assumes the entered pool is the agreed base; it does not deduct broker fees, referral fees, caps or taxes.

Calculator FAQ

What is a 70/30 commission split?

The participant assigned 70% receives $7,000 from a $10,000 pool; the other share is $3,000 before fees.

Should I enter the sale price?

No. Enter the commission amount being distributed.

Can the split exceed 100%?

No for this two-participant pool model. Use a percentage from 0 to 100.

Does it include brokerage fees or caps?

No. Apply the written agreement to determine the correct pool and any deductions.
