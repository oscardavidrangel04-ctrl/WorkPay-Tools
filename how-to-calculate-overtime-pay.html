How to Calculate Overtime Pay

Learn how to calculate overtime pay, time and a half, regular earnings and weekly gross pay with clear examples.

Reviewed September 13, 2026

Basic overtime formula

Overtime rate = regular rate × overtime multiplier

Overtime pay = overtime rate × overtime hours

Weekly gross pay = regular pay + overtime pay

Example: $20 per hour for 45 hours

Forty regular hours equal $800. Five hours at $30 time and a half equal $150. Estimated weekly gross pay is $950.

Rules and assumptions

The federal FLSA generally requires time and a half after 40 hours in a workweek for covered nonexempt workers. Eligibility, the regular rate and state requirements can vary. Review current guidance from the U.S. Department of Labor.

Frequently asked questions

How is overtime calculated at time and a half?

Multiply the regular hourly rate by 1.5, then multiply that overtime rate by overtime hours.

Is overtime always after 40 hours?

The federal rule generally uses more than 40 hours in a workweek for covered nonexempt employees, while some states provide additional rules.
