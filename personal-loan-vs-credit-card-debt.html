Personal Loan vs Credit Card Debt

A personal loan is commonly an installment obligation; credit card debt is generally revolving and may have changing required payments.

The short answer

Compare APR, fees, term, collateral, payment schedule and the consequences of using a loan to consolidate existing card balances.

What to check

Compare the current statement, agreement or offer before acting. Focus on the amount owed, APR, fees, required payment, due date and any promotional or service terms that apply to your specific accounts.
