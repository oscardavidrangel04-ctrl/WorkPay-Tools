Debt Avalanche Method: How It Works

The debt avalanche method directs extra money to the balance with the highest APR while required payments continue on other debts.

The short answer

It is an interest-focused ordering method. Verify rates, promotional terms and required payments from account records.

What to check

Compare the current statement, agreement or offer before acting. Focus on the amount owed, APR, fees, required payment, due date and any promotional or service terms that apply to your specific accounts.

How the debt avalanche sequence works

First, list the debts by APR from highest to lowest. Keep required payments current on every account. Apply the available extra amount to the highest-rate balance; when it is paid, move the same amount to the next highest APR.

This ordering can reduce interest when other facts are equal, but promotional rates and changing account terms need to be checked. Compare it with the debt snowball method.
