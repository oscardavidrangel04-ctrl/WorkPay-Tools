What Is FICA Tax on a Paycheck?

Understand the FICA label on a paycheck, its Social Security and Medicare components, and why it differs from federal income tax withholding.

The short answer

FICA is commonly used on pay stubs for Social Security and Medicare employment taxes. It is separate from federal income tax withholding, which is affected by the information on Form W-4. A paycheck can show the items under different names depending on the payroll system.

What to check

Do not assume every deduction labelled similarly has the same calculation or that the year-to-date amount is the amount from a single check. Compare the current-period amount with the year-to-date column and retain the statement if a payroll correction is needed.

Use the right record

This overview does not calculate payroll tax. Rates, wage limits and special situations can change; use current IRS guidance or a tax professional for an individual question.

Official reference

The IRS explains that employment taxes include federal income tax withholding as well as Social Security and Medicare taxes. Read the source.
