Privacy policy

Read how WorkPay Tools handles calculator inputs and site data.

Reviewed August 27, 2026

Calculator inputs are designed to be processed in your browser and do not require an account. Do not enter sensitive identifiers such as Social Security numbers. Hosting or analytics services enabled by the site owner may process standard technical information according to their own policies.

Calculator inputs

Calculator values are generally processed in your browser to produce an estimate. You should avoid entering sensitive personal information that a calculator does not request.

Browser storage

Some interactive features may use local browser storage to remember a recent calculator state. Local storage stays on the device unless your browser or an extension handles it differently, and you can clear it through browser settings.

Basic website data

Hosting or performance services may process standard technical information such as requests, device or browser details and performance measurements. This information is used to operate and improve the site.

External links

Some pages link to government or other external websites for source material. Those sites have their own privacy practices and policies.

What the calculators store locally

Some calculators use browser local storage so recent inputs or view preferences can remain available on the same device. For example, the Time Card, Overtime Pay and Hourly to Salary tools may remember recent calculator values. This browser storage is not an account and does not require you to identify yourself. You can remove it by clearing site data in your browser.

Information you should not enter

The calculators are designed for wage, salary, hours and schedule values. They do not need Social Security numbers, bank account details, passwords, payroll credentials or other sensitive identifiers. Use sample values when testing a calculator or reporting a problem.

Hosting and performance information

The website host and any enabled performance tools may process standard request information needed to deliver and secure the site, such as IP-related connection data, browser or device information, request logs and performance measurements. WorkPay Tools uses this type of information to operate the website, diagnose errors and understand technical performance.

External websites

WorkPay Tools links to official government pages and other external resources when they help explain a calculation. Following an external link takes you to a separate website with its own privacy policy, cookies and data practices. WorkPay Tools does not control those third-party practices.

Changes to this policy

This page may be updated when site features, storage behavior or service providers change. The review date at the top of the page indicates when the policy text was last checked.
