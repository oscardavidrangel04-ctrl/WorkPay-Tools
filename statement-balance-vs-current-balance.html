Credit card balances

Statement Balance vs Current Balance

Statement balance is the amount recorded when the billing cycle closed. Current balance reflects newer posted account activity. New purchases, payments, refunds and credits can make them different.

Statement vs current balance at a glance

BalanceWhat it represents
Statement balanceBalance captured at the close of the billing cycle
Current balanceMore recent posted balance after later activity
Remaining statement balanceUnpaid portion of the statement balance after applicable payments or credits
Minimum paymentMinimum amount required under the account terms by the due date

Why current balance can be higher

See how a credit card billing period separates posted activity, and what paying before the statement closes can change.

Purchases posted after the statement closed can increase current balance without changing the already-issued statement balance. Pending transactions may be displayed separately depending on the issuer.

Why current balance can be lower

Payments, refunds and credits posted after the statement closed can reduce the current balance. Check how the issuer labels remaining statement balance before scheduling another payment.

Which balance should you pay?

First distinguish the minimum payment from the statement balance. The closing date and due date also refer to different points in the billing cycle.

The answer depends on the goal and account terms. At minimum, pay the required amount on time. If the card provides a grace period and its conditions are met, paying the applicable statement balance in full by the due date can avoid purchase interest. Paying the current balance may also cover newer purchases, but it is not always necessary to satisfy the issued statement.

Official sources: CFPB grace-period guidance and CFPB credit card resources.

Statement Balance FAQ

What is remaining statement balance?

It is the portion of the issued statement balance that remains unpaid after applicable payments and credits.

Does current balance include pending purchases?

Issuer displays differ. Some show pending transactions separately until they post.

Can interest still appear after paying?

It can, depending on the grace period, carried balances, transaction type, residual interest and account agreement.

Current balance vs statement balance

The statement balance is fixed for a completed billing cycle. The current balance changes as newer purchases, payments, credits, fees and interest post. A current balance can therefore be higher or lower than the statement balance.

Related questions

Which balance should I pay to avoid purchase interest?

When a purchase grace period applies, the account terms generally focus on paying the applicable statement balance in full by the due date.

Why is my remaining statement balance different?

Payments or credits applied after the statement can reduce the remaining amount from that cycle.
