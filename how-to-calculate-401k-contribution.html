How to Calculate a 401(k) Contribution

Understand how a salary percentage becomes a planned 401(k) contribution.

The basic method

A contribution estimate multiplies eligible compensation by the elected contribution percentage. Plan rules and current legal limits can affect the real payroll amount.

What to verify

Use the written policy, statement, contract or plan document for the real terms. A simple formula cannot account for every timing rule, fee, benefit or eligibility condition.

Example and plan checks

Multiply eligible annual compensation by the contribution percentage, then divide by expected pay periods for a simple per-paycheck estimate. Verify plan eligibility, election timing and current limits in the plan materials.

Question to check

Does a contribution equal an employer match?

No. Your elected contribution and an employer match are separate amounts.
