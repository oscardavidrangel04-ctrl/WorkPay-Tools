Compensation & Benefits Tools & Guides

Compare more than base salary: bonus structures, commissions, contractor revenue targets, equity and salary bands. Each tool explains which entered values it includes; none establishes entitlement or market fair pay.

Compare the parts of a compensation offer

  1. Start with guaranteed pay: distinguish base salary from a target bonus, commission or estimated equity value.
  2. Add the other components: use the total compensation guide to keep cash, employer-paid benefits and conditional awards separate.
  3. Check when an award becomes yours: read how a vesting schedule works and compare the dates with the written offer.

Use the same time period for both offers. An annual salary, one-time signing bonus and multi-year equity grant cannot be compared by simply adding their headline amounts.
