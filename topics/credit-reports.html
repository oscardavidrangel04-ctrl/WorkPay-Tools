Credit Reports & Scores Tools & Guides

Understand report information, scoring models and how to inspect possible errors. Begin with the report-reading guide before treating a score difference or a checklist result as evidence of a specific problem.

Match the report problem to the next step

  1. You do not recognize an entry: start with how to read a credit report and identify the account, balance and reporting date.
  2. A reported fact is wrong: use the credit report error guide to organize the specific correction and supporting documents.
  3. A score differs or changes: check the factors behind a credit score before comparing numbers from different dates or models.

For a timeline question, choose the guide for that exact item. Credit inquiries, closed accounts and collection accounts are different records.
