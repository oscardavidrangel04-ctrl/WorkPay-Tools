Time & Work Hours Tools & Guides

Move from a clock-in and clock-out record to paid hours, decimal time and annual work-hour assumptions. Choose the time input you already have rather than treating every time tool as the same calculation.

Start with clock times or a recurring schedule

  1. You have clock-in and clock-out entries: use the time card calculator and enter breaks for the actual shifts.
  2. You have a repeated daily schedule: use the work-hours calculator with shift length, break minutes and days worked.
  3. You need decimal time: use hours to decimal before multiplying time by a pay rate.

For example, 7 hours 30 minutes is 7.5 hours, not 7.30. Keep paid time, elapsed time and entered unpaid breaks distinct when checking a payroll total.
