Salary & Pay Tools & Guides

Convert pay rates, compare payroll schedules and understand fixed salary versus hourly earnings. Start with the calculation that matches the numbers you know, then use a guide to interpret the result.

Choose a pay conversion that matches your offer

  1. Paid by the hour: use hourly to salary with your expected paid hours and weeks. Keep unpaid gaps separate.
  2. Quoted an annual salary: use salary to hourly to compare offers on the same work schedule.
  3. Planning around payday: compare biweekly and semimonthly pay before converting an annual amount into checks.

A monthly average and the deposits arriving in a particular month answer different questions. Use the same gross or net basis on both sides of a comparison.
