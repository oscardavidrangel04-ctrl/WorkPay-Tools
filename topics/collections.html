Collections & Debt Validation Tools & Guides

Separate credit-report disputes, debt validation, payment questions and collector contact issues. Start with the problem you are trying to resolve; paying a debt and correcting reporting are different processes.

Identify whether the issue is the debt, reporting or contact

  1. You do not recognize the collector: follow the steps to identify a collection agency and verify the contact details independently.
  2. The debt or amount is disputed: review how to document a debt that is not yours and preserve the notices and supporting records.
  3. You already paid: compare the receipt, account reference and claimed balance using the paid-debt follow-up guide.

A credit-report entry, a collector’s payment demand and a court notice need different responses. Keep their dates and account references separate when organizing documents.
