Paychecks, Taxes & Retirement Tools & Guides

Read payroll amounts, distinguish gross wages from taxable income and compare retirement contributions. This collection explains scope and record differences; it is not a state tax engine or personalized tax return.

Start with the number on your paycheck

  1. A deduction is unclear: identify the label with paycheck deductions explained, then separate income-tax withholding from FICA payroll taxes.
  2. Your W-2 differs from salary: use gross pay versus W-2 wages to compare the same year and wage definition.
  3. You are changing retirement contributions: read how traditional and Roth 401(k) contributions affect taxable income before estimating the change in your deposit.

Have the pay-period dates, gross wages, deduction labels and year-to-date totals ready. Those records make a comparison more useful than a salary figure alone.
