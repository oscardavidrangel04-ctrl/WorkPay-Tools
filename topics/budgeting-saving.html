Budgeting & Saving Tools & Guides

Build a monthly expense picture, separate recurring bills from irregular costs and model savings goals or an emergency fund. The useful starting point is an accurate entered budget, not a universal spending target.

Turn income and bills into a savings plan

  1. Find the starting budget: use the monthly bill calculator after converting annual or quarterly costs to the same monthly basis.
  2. Set a dated goal: use the monthly savings-goal calculator with the amount still needed and the months available.
  3. Separate planned costs from surprises: compare a sinking fund and an emergency fund before assigning the same savings to both.

Use take-home deposits for a spending budget, and keep the chosen income basis consistent when measuring a savings rate. A monthly average does not replace a calendar of bill due dates.
