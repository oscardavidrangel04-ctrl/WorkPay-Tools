PTO & Leave Tools & Guides

Compare accrued time off, an entered accrual rate and the gross wage value of saved PTO hours. A planning estimate does not determine payout rights, eligibility or the employer’s policy.

Choose between earned leave and its wage value

  1. Tracking hours earned: use the PTO accrual calculator with the rate and periods specified by your policy.
  2. Checking an existing balance: follow the PTO accrual guide and reconcile opening hours, earned hours and leave already used.
  3. Comparing the value of hours: use the PTO value calculator with the relevant hourly rate.

Record whether a balance is in hours or days and how many hours count as a day. The written policy and applicable rules determine carryover, caps and payout eligibility; a wage-value estimate does not resolve those terms.
