How to Calculate a Savings Rate

Calculate a monthly savings rate and understand what the simple percentage leaves out.

The basic method

A simple savings rate divides money saved during a period by gross income for the same period. Definitions of savings and income can vary, so keep periods consistent.

What to verify

Use the written policy, statement, contract or plan document for the real terms. A simple formula cannot account for every timing rule, fee, benefit or eligibility condition.

Simple example

Saving $600 from $5,000 of gross monthly income produces a 12% simple savings rate. The result changes if you use take-home pay instead, so label the income definition you choose.

Question to check

Why use gross income?

It creates a consistent planning denominator, but a net-income approach can also be useful if used consistently.
