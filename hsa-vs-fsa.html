HSA vs FSA: Basic Differences to Check

Understand the basic differences between an HSA and FSA, including eligibility and ownership questions to verify with official materials.

The short answer

A health savings account (HSA) and flexible spending arrangement (FSA) can both help pay eligible health expenses, but they have different eligibility, contribution and ownership rules. An HSA requires eligibility tied to a qualifying high-deductible health plan and other conditions. An FSA is typically an employer-sponsored arrangement.

What to check

Do not assume unused money follows the same rule in each account. Carryover, grace-period and forfeiture rules can vary, and HSA ownership differs from many employer arrangements. Read the plan materials before selecting a contribution amount.

Use the right record

This guide is a comparison checklist, not tax advice. Confirm eligibility, contribution limits, qualified expenses and deadlines using current IRS resources and your employer plan documents.

Official reference

The IRS explains the eligibility and rules for health savings accounts and flexible spending arrangements in its current publications. Read the source.
