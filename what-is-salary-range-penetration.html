What Is Salary Range Penetration?

Learn how salary range penetration describes a pay amount within a stated range.

The basic method

Salary range penetration compares a salary with a range minimum and maximum. It is a compensation-planning measure, not a determination of fair pay or an employer obligation.

Worked example and comparison with compa-ratio

Suppose a salary band runs from $60,000 to $100,000 and the salary is $70,000. Range penetration is ($70,000 − $60,000) ÷ ($100,000 − $60,000) × 100 = 25%. The salary is one-quarter of the way from the minimum to the maximum.

Why this is not a compa-ratio

The midpoint of that band is $80,000. Dividing the $70,000 salary by the midpoint gives a compa-ratio of 87.5%, not 25%. The two metrics answer different questions and should not share a label.

  • Use salary and band values on the same annual or hourly basis.
  • Use the appropriate job level, location and current band.
  • If maximum equals minimum, the penetration formula is undefined.
  • The raw formula can be negative below the minimum or above 100% beyond the maximum.

Neither metric alone determines an appropriate raise or evaluates job performance.

Simple interpretation

A salary near the range minimum has lower penetration than a salary near the maximum. The metric is useful for internal range discussion but does not decide promotion, merit or market value on its own.

Question to check

What formula is commonly used?

A basic form is salary minus range minimum, divided by range maximum minus range minimum.
