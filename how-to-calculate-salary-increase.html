How to Calculate a Salary Increase

Calculate a salary increase using percentage and dollar formulas, with worked examples for annual, monthly, biweekly and hourly pay.

Reviewed September 13, 2026

Salary increase formulas

Raise amount = current salary × (raise percentage ÷ 100)

New salary = current salary + raise amount

Increase percentage = ((new salary − old salary) ÷ old salary) × 100

Worked example: 5% raise on $60,000

  1. Convert 5% to 0.05.
  2. Multiply $60,000 × 0.05 = $3,000.
  3. Add $3,000 to $60,000 for a new salary of $63,000.

Gross raise versus take-home raise

A salary increase calculator normally reports gross pay. Your net paycheck change can be smaller because withholding, benefit contributions and other deductions may also change.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a 10% raise?

Multiply the current salary by 0.10 and add that result to the current salary.

How can I calculate the raise when I know both salaries?

Subtract the old salary from the new salary, divide by the old salary and multiply by 100.
