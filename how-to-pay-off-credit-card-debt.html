How to Pay Off Credit Card Debt: A Planning Guide

Start with current statement balances, APRs, due dates and required payments; then decide how much extra money is available for payoff.

The short answer

Keep required payments current, confirm promotional rates and fees, and use the card issuer statement as the source for account-specific terms.

What to check

Compare the current statement, agreement or offer before acting. Focus on the amount owed, APR, fees, required payment, due date and any promotional or service terms that apply to your specific accounts.

Credit card debt payoff checklist

  • Record the current balance and purchase APR from every statement.
  • Confirm the required payment and due date before allocating extra money.
  • Check whether a promotional rate, balance-transfer fee or deferred-interest term applies.
  • Use a debt payoff plan to choose an order and the payoff calculator to model one balance.
