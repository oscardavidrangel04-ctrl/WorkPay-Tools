What Affects a Credit Score? Reported Account Factors

Credit scores use report information, but the result depends on the model, report data and timing.

The short answer

Payment history, balances relative to limits, account age, new credit activity and account mix are commonly discussed factors.

What to review

Focus on accurate report data and actual account terms. Do not assume that one percentage guarantees a score result.
