Salary terminology

What Is Prorated Salary?

Prorated salary is a proportional amount of salary paid for only part of a full period. It may arise when employment starts or ends mid-period or another partial-period rule applies.

Reviewed September 15, 2026

Prorated salary formula

Prorated pay = full-period salary × eligible portion of the period

A workday approach uses payable workdays divided by scheduled workdays. If full semimonthly gross pay is $2,500 and 6 of 10 workdays are payable, estimated prorated pay is $1,500.

How to calculate prorated salary or pro rata pay

  1. Find full gross salary for the pay period.
  2. Count scheduled workdays using the employer's calendar.
  3. Determine how many workdays are payable.
  4. Multiply full-period salary by the payable fraction.

The Prorated Salary Calculator performs this workday calculation.

Why methods can differ

Payroll systems may use workdays, calendar days, annual working days or another permitted convention. Holidays, paid leave, rounding and the reason for the partial period can affect the result. A mathematical estimate does not determine whether a salary deduction is legally permitted.

Prorated salary FAQ

Is prorated salary gross or net?

Proration normally calculates a gross-pay amount before taxes and deductions.

How do I calculate prorated salary for a partial month?

Use full-period gross salary multiplied by the payable share of the period, following the selected payroll day-count method.

Should I use calendar days or workdays?

Use the method required by the employer's payroll practice and applicable rules. Do not mix the two conventions.

Can bonuses be prorated?

A bonus plan may define its own eligibility and proration method, separate from salary.
