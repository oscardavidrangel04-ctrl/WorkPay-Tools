Income terminology

Gross Income vs Annual Income

Gross income describes income before deductions. Annual income describes income measured over one year. An amount can therefore be both annual and gross—for example, gross annual income.

Gross vs annual income at a glance

TermWhat it describes
Gross incomeIncome before applicable deductions
Annual incomeIncome measured across a year
Gross annual incomeAnnual income before deductions
Net annual incomeAnnual income remaining after applicable deductions

Gross annual income example

An employee earning $25 per hour for 40 hours per week and 52 paid weeks has estimated gross annual employment income of $52,000 before taxes and deductions. Use the Annual Income Calculator for different schedules.

Annual salary vs annual income

Annual salary is fixed employment pay stated for a year. Annual income can be broader and may include wages, salary, commissions, bonuses or other income, depending on the question and document. Keep the included sources explicit.

Gross income vs net income

Gross is measured before deductions; net is measured afterward. See gross pay and net pay for paycheck-specific terminology.

Gross vs Annual Income FAQ

Is annual income the same as gross income?

Not always. Annual is a period; gross is a before-deduction measure.

What is gross annual income?

It is total yearly income before applicable taxes and deductions.

Does annual income mean salary?

Not necessarily. Salary can be one part of annual income.
