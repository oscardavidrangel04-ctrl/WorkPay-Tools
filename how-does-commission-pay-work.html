How Does Commission Pay Work?

Commission pay ties some or all cash earnings to sales or another defined result. The plan determines what counts, when commission is earned and which percentage or tier applies.

Reviewed October 9, 2026

How to calculate sales commission

Commission = eligible sales × commission rate

At 5% on $25,000 of eligible sales, commission is $1,250. Use the Commission Calculator to add optional base pay for the same period.

Salary plus commission and commission-only structures

  • Commission only: cash earnings primarily depend on eligible results.
  • Salary plus commission: fixed base pay plus variable commission.
  • Tiered commission: the rate changes after defined thresholds.
  • Draw against commission: an advance may later be reconciled with earned commission.
  • Gross-margin commission: commission is based on margin rather than sales revenue.

Questions to ask about a commission plan

  • Which sales are eligible and when are they credited?
  • Is commission earned at booking, payment or another event?
  • Can returns or cancellations create chargebacks?
  • Are there quotas, tiers, caps or accelerators?
  • How are shared accounts and departures handled?

Flat commission example vs marginal tiers

At 5% of $40,000 in eligible sales, commission is $2,000. A marginal tier plan could instead pay 3% on the first $20,000 and 6% on the next $20,000: $600 plus $1,200, or $1,800. A plan applying 6% retroactively to all $40,000 would produce $2,400. The tier wording therefore matters.

When commission is earned is not always when it is paid

A sale, signed contract, invoice or collected customer payment can be different events. Ask which event the plan uses and how refunds or cancellations are handled. A draw may be an advance rather than extra earnings; whether it is recoverable depends on its terms and applicable requirements. See bonus vs commission when comparing a sales-linked payment with a separate target award.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a flat sales commission?

Multiply eligible sales by the commission percentage divided by 100. For example, $40,000 times 5% gives $2,000.

Do all tiered plans apply the higher rate to all sales?

No. Some apply each rate only to the sales inside its tier; others use a retroactive rate. Read the actual plan.

What is salary plus commission?

A structure combining base salary with commission earned under specified conditions. It is not the same as a guaranteed target payout.

Does commission-only mean overtime rules do not apply?

No. Pay structure alone does not determine overtime eligibility or an exemption.
